Scripture Reading: First letter of Peter to the dispersed tribes.

Greetings, beloveds, It is well when you are prepared within as you have been with prayer and meditation. Now you are better able to receive the higher truths of Spirit, for your minds and emotions are stilled. When you give up your ways and your will and come before your Lord with the focus on His purpose, then you transcend all else and come as a prepared vessel, to be filled with more of God's Holy Spirit. In these last days of the Age you are coming face-to-face with many things that will surprise and shock you. You are being told this ahead of time and you are being ministered unto by the angels of Heaven. You are being guided and led through the valleys of deceit by no other than the Shepherd of the flock, the Lord who has been given authority to govern the entire world as well as other flocks. And it has taken a long time to help you to come into position, into the realization that you are, even on this very day, dealing with the presence, the power of your Lord. It should not be surprising, for it was promised by the Father, but the words have fallen on deaf ears for a long time and you had to be conditioned to take the increase of light.

The body is not accustomed to receiving as much power as you have been experiencing in your midst during these past three weeks. It is like supplying you with food for the physical body, or fuel for vehicles which carry you in transportation. The needs of the spiritual body to meet what is to take place have been mercifully provided for by the Holy Spirit of God. You have benefitted by that magnification of power here and have received some conscious benefits.

But most of it you were not aware of. It must be done; the nourishment of the soul is essential. Your faith in and love for the Master Jesus, who fulfilled this plan for restoring souls to renewed life, have brought this about. The desire to be one with your Lord has tremendous power and opens the door, opens the com-partments of the consciousness so that finally you come into one great, expanded awareness. The reason that we expand on different subjects from time to time is that as you grow and open we can come back with a little more on those same subjects. One of them has been the meaning of the great sacrifice on the cross; it has been difficult for you to understand, Another was when God spoke to Adam and Eve and said, "Of the fruit of this tree you must not eat or you will surely die." He meant the distraction and the deceits of the world. Even now, if you slide back nye the various aspects of the world kingdom, and your motives changing accordingly, then you are again eating of the fruits of the false systems-- the dishonesty, the greed, the selfishness, the hate, the lust.

Today, the business world is coming more and more under the domination of Lucifer, and soul energies are being dissipated and diluted. They were when the children of Israel, who were told to keep the race pure, intermarried with other racese The race was to be kept pure and separate from the world for God's purposes, but not because of racial superiority. Many remained true to this; many did not. Today man is forcing integration and intermarriage among those whom God has willed to be separate peoples, with each according to the purposes in His

So began the restoration of those souls into balance, into purpose, € into fullness of life. God has been working through this, waiting patiently for those to come forth. Mankind, working through layers and layers of materiality and deceit, with so many karmic debts upon his shoulders, could not have risen if left to himself, for there were not enough of the Divine Energies left him, due to the dilution and his unbelief. There was no longer power in their coming together under the law of Oneness.

And so in a cave, unspoiled and apart from man's motives and greeds, the Child was born. And God's power was established upon Earth once One came with the capacity and love, and assumed a role for a time. He carried the burden of all karmic debt upon his shoulders, so that man could rise and be free. This did not relieve man of the responsibility of working out the remainder that he was left to cope with in good faith and an attitude of love, but he was no longer trapped. He was given the key; he was shown how it could be used, how divine love could give him the power to transcend the remainder and go through unto the resurrection. But he had to believe it, identify with that lawein-action and move with it. If he claimed it and gave thanks, he was forgiven and strengthened, cleansed to a point and it would continue. As he became aware he was told to forgive himself. Once he knew not; now he knows in an entirely different perspective. Now he believes in the One the Father sent to help man, to help him become integrated once more with the spiritual powers and the nature in which he was sent forth, to help him become disentangled from the webs of the world kingdom. And finally, the entire Earth would be cleansed.
