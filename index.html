About Louise

Louise had a life of devotion to all things spiritual. She was a life long church goer, attending several different churches in her life. She had a series of contacts with the divine starting as a child long before she began to ‘channel’ messages from the celestial beings. One I remember her telling was of her growing up in Texas in the country. She was a little girl being raised by disciplinary parents. She was put in a closet once for something she did or said. When there, she saw that she was sharing the closet with a rattle snake. Then an angel or being appears and calmed the rattle snake so it didn’t strike. When her mother opened the door to let her out, she passed out seeing the rattle snake. Louise then went and got her father who came and dealt with the snake. This is one of many stories she told of “life in the spirit” as she put it.

When she began to channel, she had an active prayer life often hearing the words of the master as a response to her prayer. I am going to shorten this part to say that she was visiting the A.R.E. to see what was going on and following a trail of discovery on the spiritual path.

While sitting with friends one night, they all saw lights in the sky which they realized was a large mother ship with several smaller ships coming and going from. After watching for some time and late at night, she was the last one up. She heard steps coming up the walk and opening the door to find a luminous being in front of her. They “talked” through telepathy and she was asked to be a channel of their messages. She was told to pray about it they would return another day for her answer. They described themselves as part of the host of heaven coming back with Jesus. Part of a large group of celestial beings waiting around the earth both waiting and helping people who want to grow spiritually as well as preventing catastrophic destruction of the earth due to such things as nuclear weapons. There is much to share of her but I will leave it there. Welcome to the readings of Louise Morse called the Portals of Light Ministry.
