## CHANNELLING AND GROUP VORTEX

Many forget that there is a Plan that was laid out long ago by the great Architect, much greater than your human mind could foresee. You must not be discouraged when the picture does not move for you as you have envisioned, for you do see with the finite mind; and beloveds, only the infinite can truly create. That is the beauty in accepting the great Plan and moving with only the desire to go according to the Divine Will, for now you see blindly, and it is your only measuring stick for a few years. Is it not so that you have had eons of existence, and that you have been cared for by your Father through these many eons? So why should you doubt for these few years that you can measure?

There is purpose in all. You have come, beloveds, to a time of correcting, of returning to the point from which you first strayed in this life that you may correct the errors of an immature spiritual being, for now you have grown into a deeper understanding. Your Spirit has awakened; it is no longer dormant. Therefore, you must move into the next path of your enlightenment and your service.

In order to do this much has to be corrected, and you must remove those things or individuals who have become a barrier to the path, not only of your growth and development but most of all in your service to your Lord. For true it is that you can work with and love and forgive many individuals along the way, that you may serve better in finding your individual path, that you may surrender all to your Maker and not withhold any part of your being or desires or purposes.

You are told not to accept all spirits that come, but only those that come in the Name of the Christ consciousness, the work and mission of the Christ, and those that come in the Spirit of Truth. Your are told to test the spirits to see if they are of God, and that you must always be careful.

When a group conscientiously dedicates itself for years and years with pure aims and thoughts, and it is seeking only the highest Truth of God, then it is receiving in that Light and in that state of consciousness to which it is attuned. If one of low consciousness should by chance come near, they could not stand the Light of the Christ that surrounds. Immediately they are recognized. There have been very few coming near this group in years past, and they were quickly dispersed. They were recognized as such because you have insulation; you have the guidance and the protection of the Spirits and Angels of Christ. That's the difference. That's the danger of going into places of fortune-telling and people who only seek that of low level, for they are not insulated, they are not devoted to the Christ consciousness or to the Light of God. They are not seeking that, and that makes the difference.

There are the familiar spirits, those who have lived on Earth and are still around the Earth. They have a greater vision than you do, but they don't have all Truth. That's why we don't know all the answers to give you. We are channels, and many things come down, channelled down through us; and when we have great Truth, it is channelled down to us. We don't know it from this region, but we are being used as instruments and channels just like mediums on Earth are. We open to the Truth and teachings that come through us, and we give ourselves to the work of the Christ. That is our mission.

When sitting in a group sometimes in going toward the trance state, a feeling as of falling asleep may preceed this. And one not recognizing it for what it is sometimes unconsciously fights it by resisting and pulling back and forth; this brings discomfort. When you learn to let go, know that you are protected by the very desire of your soul, that you are putting yourself in the hands of the Divine, and that you are letting go in the knowledge that you are safe in body, mind, and soul. When you put yourself in the keeping of those coming in the Name of the Christ and working in the mission of the Father, do not be afraid. Do not feel that you must hold to your intellect and your will and your power and your logic. Use these, but at the time that you are working with the Spirit you do not need to hold on, for God will sustain you. Let yourself drift into the unknown, and be not afraid. We are with you, we will bring many things to you, and disclose the beauties of the heavens to those that can turn loose the earthly. Be patient and it will come in greater ways.

At times there are those who put hindrances in the way, some things pertaining to the strong will of the human. Let the will rest and abide in the will of God.

The image of the Father is deep within each one of the Sons of God. Jesus did say that all things that He did, you could do also and greater things than He, for the Divine Image of the Father is buried within each of you. But you have turned away. He did not turn away; He followed through, and His will became One with the Father. That is what He was trying to show. And the moment that your will becomes One with the Father, you will be in complete alignment and harmony, and all things will be done through you. There will not be the blocking, the interference, the shutting off of the divine. Then you will be one free channel.

Whether it is a prayer group or a group like this, your vibrations change, your chemistry changes every time you sit together. When people sit only for psychic phenomena, they open up just the astral realm, but they do not open up to the high Spirits. The ethereal is what you want. So you receive, and as you receive, freely give. You are a channel too. It is not a matter of being selective or of one being of higher consciousness or living a better life than another. This is not to be judged. It is whether or not you have the doors open. You may open one and close another at the same time. You may desire to love, yet you may resent your neighbor because he has mistreated you, so your prayer is cancelled out. Now we would not say that it does no good, but we would say that there cannot be the open passage. If you attempt to speak over your telephone and the line is cut, you may speak all day and praise yourself that you have spoken, but the message has not reached very far except in your own ears.

You must learn to open the door through sacrificing the things that you might like within yourself, these that are as habit-forming in the human consciousness. Many would be quite unhappy if they could not resent their neighbor or be a little bitter because they have been mistreated. It would take away the joy which they have. This is human. You must learn to find joy through more satisfactory means. You must lift your sights. You must open the doors and sacrifice the petty things, the negative reactions of the emotions so that the channel be completely open. Then let your soul pour forth a desire unto God, and it is created.

The answer is yours before you have asked in the way that makes for all good, not necessarily in the way that the finite mind might picture it. You open the doors to the wisdom of the Father, not to the finite knowledge. If you had all wisdom within you, you would not need to ask of infinity, but in the conscious mind you do not have it. You do have it in the image of God within, but you have closed the door there long ago. So that is the door we are opening.

You will eventually have your wisdom, your answers, your love, and all needs met through the power of God within your being, your Holy Temple. You will not have to seek from teachers, or channels, or ethereal beings, or anyone other than the God within you. We are leading you into this. It is the reason we do not answer all your questions, do not make your decisions for you, do not take your responsibility away from you. We would do you harm if we did this. We are leading you into the Light, and teaching you to walk in the Spirit that you have communion with your Father and have all things unto you.

In this understanding of perfection, it is the going toward the perfected Plan which is all Light, all wisdom, all good. And the perfection in the soul of the individual is not what man calls "perfection" (perfect in the eyes of man). Many justify themselves by saying, "We are human, we cannot be perfect." Perfect is not what you think it is. You do have the potentials. You have the plan within you which is the completion, the plan of perfection, even as the acorn holds the perfect plan of the mighty oak - the growth, the looks, the form, the age - the perfect plan and rhythm and cycle of growth of the great oak. It is the plan of perfection that is fulfilled when the acorn releases the plan within it, and the oak begins to grow tall and straight before its Creator, reaching a certain height, certain proportions, certain life cycles, then appearing to die. It has completed the cycle of that purpose which was intended for it, the completed cycle including birth, growth, life, and change or transformation. It is a completed plan, a perfected plan.

Perfection is that entirety, that completion of the thought and purpose, the idea sent forth from God, a plan for that life or that race or that universe, a completed plan, a cycle of a Divine Plan. You are becoming perfected. You are in the midst of a great Divine Plan. You are not perfect yet because the Plan has not been completed. When you find yourself to be what you call "unworthy", not having reached high attainment, you call it lack of perfection, imperfection, and you look down on yourself. This is injustice. You do not look down upon a soul that is in the process of blooming into full blossom. You are walking in a Plan; you are living; you are becoming aware of a small segment of that Plan. You are part of a great Divine Plan of the universe, a Plan for the Sons of God. You are growing in this, becoming perfected.

Any law in any dimension is but a part and a reflection in that dimension of the greater spiritual law which was given at the Source. Are not these laws then used to transcend the physical, to lift you into higher vibration, to open the doors of the potentials within you, the dormant state within? Man must come out of his weakness, his inferiority as he calls it, and recognize the truth of himself. Humanly he is nothing, even as the Master, but as he recognizes his identity and he recognizes that the potentials, the attributes of God are within him, he removes his fear and his lack of confidence within himself, his anxiety, his worry, his possessiveness of things of Earth, or of families of Earth. Parents sometimes possess their children, many become possessive in relationships.

Then would man find that a new courage and strength would come from the deep wellspring within, that immortal one. In quietness and in confidence shall be your strength. It has been given that from deep within the center of your being, the Kingdom of God within you, shall come your courage, your confidence, your strength, and your overcoming. Seek it there. Put down self that the Divine may work through you.

All are channels to one degree or another, but many are the channels of Earth who have been prepared over many lifetimes to be used as vessels and instruments, because they have learned to set aside themselves for a greater part of a lifetime that the Father may work through them in many mysterious ways. It is not for man to question which way, for all things of the Spirit seem as foolishness to the eyes of the world. Each may choose that which he would follow, but the world shall crumble in the physical sense, in the material sense. True reality is of the spiritual nature of man and life in the cosmic realm.

As you become highly sensitized and closer to living in other dimensions for greater lengths of time, you shall find that you understand concepts better, and eventually you shall be transmuted. But in the interim comes your tribulation, for it shall appear to be worse as you enter into the higher realms, many times it shall seem an agony.

This was also presented in example by the Master Jesus even as He went through persecution, misunderstandings, torture, even physical death, crucifixion, on through to the grave. The Master went through all vibrations down to the lowest, down into Beelzebub's realm, overcame the grave, entered into the interior of the Earth to the lowest vibration that He might bring that part of Light and lift those that would follow. With the Light through each vibration on back to the ascension, He brought new life where there was death. Each individual shall go through the wilderness, as it is called, the valleys; you cannot grow without this.

There seems to be inequality in the Earth at the moment because of the past incarnations. There are those who have made use of the opportunities of progress, those who have not and have wasted incarnations in a sense. Therefore, many who have had intentions and motives that were not of purity, now in this incarnation have many difficulties, even as you find in other lands, in other countries. You cannot judge nor say that the mercy of God is not present, for even so He raises them from this and gives opportunities not only through many incarnations, but later through many planes of consciousness. All are given opportunity, but they do not all reach the same degree at the same time.

As the awakened ones come into a greater understanding, become more receptive, they open the doors to negative forces that see the effecttiveness, see that they are approaching the greater realization, and try to defeat the purpose of the soul and mislead it again as they did in the Paradise state. Was not even the Master Jesus tempted many times and persecuted? All who follow shall necessarily go through this time of tribulation, because you cannot reach the heights without going through the lowest, through the valleys on to the mountain top. THEN SHALL YOU TRULY OVERCOME; then shall you know and recognize.

Thus it is that as the meek have reached a point in their evolving and in their incarnations, they have learned to subdue certain parts, they have overcome to where they are not dictatorial, they have reached a place of learning and yet may have gone to the other extreme by not understanding. As they focus upon meekness or humility or even their lack of confidence, the inferiority, then they are truly focusing upon the self, and this will eventually pass.

They must look upon self to see it for what it is. Have you thought of this? They must be ready to face self, to see the reflection, to look in the mirror of truth, come through it, overcome it, choose, bring all into alignment, and finally overcome and ascend. But as each one is going through periods of sensitivity, there will be greater travail, greater pain and agony, even appearing to be failing in the eyes of many, to most of the world. Even Jesus failed as seen through the eyes of the world.

Turn within, try to understand yourself, your motives, your purposes and aims; work with them and yet have a certain contentment and acceptance while you are doing this. You must not be ruffled and disturbed. The preparation for the days ahead will come to you through this inner revelation.

Long ago the Master Jesus tried to teach man that all that He did man could also, for He allowed the Father to do the works through Him. If one is willing to open completely and offer himself to his God, he can become a channel, a vessel for service through that manifestation of creation. You do not have to do anything in the same way as another. This is a mistake. Many try to be like another. Be yourself, an individual spark of God, of life made in HIs image with free will to individually express the Creator upon all the planes that you inhabit. You can always improve self, yes, but be the individual self that you are. Do not try to imitate another.

All the Sons of God in their turn of readiness will be helped to turn loose of Guardians at times and begin to walk upon Earth in the Spirit, in the discernment of the Divine Spirit within. More and more shall be brought through. It is more important that it come forth from the Divine Self within than from other teachers from without.

This will eventually be true of you and many. Patience and forbearance for discernment must be, and the Spirit must move in the strength of love and compassion for those of Earth. Many things shall come through even in the conscious state, for that is where the practice comes in.

Overcoming and ascension is something that is offered right now to many upon Earth. They may go in a drifting manner over the Earth the rest of their days, or they may find a goal, a mission that they may start to accomplish and gain and make great strides in overcoming much that had not been overcome before. Forgiveness is one thing, but overcoming weaknesses - areas that have not been fully developed - is something else. The overcoming of karma and forgiveness and your freedom were one thing, and that of overcoming weaknesses and growing is another.

The Master had reached the ultimate in unselfish Divine Love, for He had the desire and never lost sight of returning to the Source. Then reaching an incorruptible state, He came back when He did not have a requirement to do this, but He volunteered in love to give His brothers a helping hand.

This has been the key to the manifesting of God through Earth. Many think that the holding of the recognition of God within them is completely sufficient, but in a group the Light around them is intensified, the power and strength is intensified. Why would it be given, "Where two or three are gathered together in My Name, there am I in the midst of them? There is great power, and in that Spirit and understanding and identification with the Name and Nature of God, the Sons of God, brought together for one purpose, can accomplish all things.

With this great love and power, He came as an example, and all who would serve as channels of God must recognize that in the manifestations of all His creation. In all dimensions do you see and find your God, whether it be through the smile of one, the hand clasp of another, the love within the heart, the belief, the faith, the sustaining of each other. In this sense you are your brother's keeper, not imposing your will upon him, not becoming possessive, not being bound to his domination, but free to love in the divine sense, to serve your God by serving your fellowman. The touch of the hands of God's instrument is the touch of God to the finite recognition. The simple ways of love in the soul response to the need of your fellowman need no other motive than to express God to him through you as you get out of the way, and let this come forth. The understanding of the heart in need brings response even as the love of the blind for those that help them to see. Letting others see the Christ Light in you is for that moment being "God" to them. This was taken for blasphemy as the Master gave it, for many did not understand.

God manifests in sunsets of the skies, in the mountains, the trees, all nature, the beauties of the planets, the solar systems He has created, but most of all, in the very heart of Him, the projection of the begotten Sons who would be His instruments, His hands and feet upon the physical plane or upon all dimensions in the frequency in which He would manifest, trusting His begotten to represent the visible part of Him.

Are you true to that trust? Do you take His Name in vain? Do you have compassion and forgive, or do you pass judgment? And do you feel free to walk in the Spirit according to the soul conviction within you, not being bound to any human being upon Earth but following the way your God leads?

Bless you, beloveds. May you follow the path shown by the Master.
