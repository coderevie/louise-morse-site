The Holy Writings are so simple when read by the soul in the spirit. It is only when the mortal mind begins to try to figure out and to analyze in all the ways of the mental that it becomes confused. Jesus gave all His teachings in simplicity using paralles of things on the earth that could help them to see by seeing a parallel of something on earth and they could understand a little more some of the higher laws that were being shown. So in patience, in trust and love, in quietness, in confidence, shall come your strength as your Holy Writings give you. So live according to these and learn to find more time to commune with God. You do not have to call Him in any special kind or method of meditation. Just talk to your Father in heaven and to your Elder Brother, Jesus, the Shepherd of the flock that He is coming to receive. The great divine love shall encompass all and that is the power that shall lift. The more you love God with all your heart, mind and soul and others - your neighbors as yourself. Your neighbors are really the flock following Jesus - those that are going in the same direction and are a part of His body, His Church and you will become more and more like Him as you walk and talk with Him. That is what He came to earth to do, to redeem you, to save your souls from death, to have the companionship with you all praising the Father, giving unto Him the glory. Before long you will have other sightings here of ships in this area too. It is more and more over the world, but in this country many sightings in the southwest and eventually there will be more landings. And those that are attuned to them have no fear, know that God is bringing it about. They will have more experiences with them, then they can be trusted. For man is so full of fear and wars and anything he sees coming out of the skies he either wants to shoot it himself, or he is fearful that they are coming to get him. He has forgotten the meaning of love and peace with Jesus the Christ.

Those who are coming to help and serve God are coming in love, divine love, desiring to do what they can in preparation for they will come with Jesus as He said that He would come with great hosts, not armies, but hosts, multitudes of those coming with Him in obedience to the Father's plans. So be thankful, learn to be calm. And that is hard to do when you are filled and surrounded by noise. But you will draw apart. So this concludes your lesson. You could only take so much at a time and retain it. We do give you our blessings in God's name for He is great and mighty and loves all of His creation. Selah

So be it dear ones. Aleph also stands by in love and blessings for more and more are the heavenly fleets drawing near in readiness when Jesus the Great Commander in Chief gives the word, all will move into action. So get more of your things in readiness for whatever you would do toward closing out. This does not mean a day or two, but it is soon. No one knows the exact day and hour that the Lord will come. He has given the signs and the signals and they are all in order. Most of the prophecies have already been fulfilled for those that will be before His coming. So do not be concerned about the exact time, but be ready when you know it is soon. Turn loose the dependence of things upon the earth. Do not cling, or lean or put all of your dependence on things or people. He only is to be completely trusted. But He has made all available to all those that seek His Name. Many are having soul flights at night and being taught many things. Seldom do they remember when they return. Some do, but it is not necessary for when your soul leaves the body it is going to be the soul and that which it knows that will remain with you.

My children, in love do I walk with you. Did I not promise that I would never forsake you. Obey the Father in heaven for His wisdom is great and He will care for all. It is drawing close to the end of time as you know it and in this world as you know it, but be not saddened by this for it must be in order to go on into the higher realms of heaven. Did I not say long ago, "Seek ye first the Kingdom of Heaven, the Kingdom of God within you and all else shall be provided. Let your temple be filled with the words of the Holy Scriptures and let the spirit within you lead and guide you and teach you all things. Was not this a promise that the Holy Spirit would come and bring things to remembrance, teach you all things, walk with you unto the end and I walk beside you, never fear. Be quiet within your soul, learn to quiet your mortal mind and your fleshly bodies that they not be interference to the progress of the soul. Take authority in the spirit as I have shown you before. Let the spirit rule within you, grow in strength, in love, practice obedience unto the Heavenly Father. Let the oil be in your lamps. Do not delay for when the time comes, just as in the parable of long ago, there will be no time then to rush out to get it. Now is the time to be filling your soul with the words given by God throughout the cen turies. Let the Holy Spirit that is abiding within you lead you and teach you and guide you. But you be obedient unto God the Father, with no excuses. I walk with you through the meadows, and the cool waters, through the valley of the shadow of death in all areas of your life, for love is stronger than anything that can come against it and believe my children in love, give up your being unto God, to Him be the glory. Bless you dear ones, I will never leave you or forsake you.
