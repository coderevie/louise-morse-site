It would be far better had you stayed outside if you did not intend to obey. But obedience must not be to some dogma or doctrine or creed of man, but to your God and to that which He gave you in your hearts and in the preserved "Writings" that the Master gave you in parable form. Read them more often. Identify with them. Read them with the soul, not the intellect. Step into line more and more by desiring, by obeying the commandments. Love thy God first of all, above all else, with all thy being; and then love thy neighbor as thyself. In love you expand. If you are narrow, unloving, resentful, difficult in your ways, inconsiderate of others, then the expansion stops and one begins to shrivel and become warped, self-centered. When you are loving, God-centered, you expand, you make a bargain with Life. Consciousness is great light expanding, love expanding and lifting the form which carries the light. God dwells in His holy temple. As you walk forward, He doeth the works through you.

Put God first. Do as the Master did. He walked with his disciples at times, but then he would leave them and go off alone for hours and days at a time, to talk with his God, to be replenished in spirit by the angels. During those times he did not rely on himself in the physical sense. Yet there were other times when he definitely needed, and asked for, the sustaining of the twelve. And they fell asleep in his time of greatest need; they depended on him to do all by himself. But he needed their sustaining, even though he had angels ministering unto him on that level of vibration in which he was functioning.

Many of these things are not understood by the human mind. Faith must increase that the divine nature come into action. Then will come freedom, independence in the life of spirit--freedom from limitation. The lack of faith in the disciples brought many rebukes from the Master but he gave them further opportunities to test their faith.

Man literally has to give up, in the human sense, give up the will, be disillusioned with himself and with the faults of the world. Then as he recognizes his own naive and gullible state, shall see how he was led into deceit and disobedience. And he shall be humbled. Then he shall accept God's forgiveness, God's mercy and love, and come in new strength to gain a new perspective. He shall rise to greater heights, for God in him can then work and function. Man keeps wondering, "Why can't I heal? Why can't I do this and have that?" Because he is still trying to do these things in his human will. Surrender and be free! Surrender in attitude. <u>Be</u> the attitudes of the Divine Nature. You do not quite know how, or you think you do not. But again, it is giving up; it is not using your energy to push. Relax, release, repent and surrender. Then reclaiming the divine nature from which you fell. To put it another way, we might say it is as though you have become a split personality--a house divided against itself, pulling one way and then the other, until finally the divine nature became dormant. But all the potentials are there awaiting release when you make up your mind to come out of the old habits, the old attitudes, the old reactions and the holding to everything you think so dear on Earth.

Not one thing shall you carry with you into the Kingdom except a heart of love for your God--your desire, above all, for His Kingdom of Spirit--being willing to make the change no matter how different it may seem to you. Your desire will lift your consciousness. In your prayers you desire love for God in an unselfish way. You do not seek information from on High so as to gain more advantage in the third dimension. This is not the motive for seeking your God. Seek ye first the Kingdom of God--the Kingdom of Heaven in you, the God Intelligence in you. Go to this God-center. Then you shall begin to receive, marveling all the while.

Daily, men's wills are clashing over the Earth (just as swords), each to prove himself stronger than the other. Even those desiring the Kingdom of Heaven are still claiming both kingdoms, but God shall move through the Earth. Do not wait until you die, and do not wait until the cataclysms to begin to live in the higher consciousness. Do it now. And practice staying in that consciousness, holding your attitudes there. You can control your thoughts; you can go from the gutter to the sublime, in your thoughts, and anywhere in-between. Why settle for small talk when you can speak of heavenly things?
