Our greetings to you again; we are constantly with you. We desired that the message from your Holy Writings be read to you tonight, for we would direct you to that which God has preserved as instructions for you for this day. You are the same followers of the Christ with the same problems, the same human natures--the same souls being lifted up out of that human nature. So the letters that were brought through the early apostles were God-given. Thus it is brought to your remembrance as it is directed again unto your souls, calling you together once more, calling you to remember that which you have been, are, and will be.

Every sentence that was read could just as well have been said by us directly to your souls, for this message is the one that had already been given by your Lord, directed through the early ones who in this Age are now fulfilling some of the same purposes. So it is like going back in your early family life, identifying with certain of the early days and relationships. You become attuned again. You pick up where you left off in consciousness. It is drawing all that into the consciousness of the third dimension today, all your experiences, needs and purposes, bringing them through to the surface. Time is being telescoped. This message is just as real as it would be if the Lord had delivered it through Peter today, as real now as then.

There is new power added when it is read anew to you and moving through in what you might call electrical impulses, the spiritual impulses of light moving into the very cells of your being through the voice vibration, through the content of the message. Light is penetrating and entering your soul bodies, as though you were being fed by injection. But you are being fed by projection. It becomes substance in you as you are raised in consciousness, as you are led point by point, and as we do the best that we can to raise you up into the constancy of the higher focus where the Lord leads us all. Come under that guidance of His pervading spirit.

You are receiving in many more ways than you realize as you sit quietly in this prepared atmosphere and setting, not only in this beautiful place of nature, not only in the circle of warm-hearted and loving friends, souls opening to receive with you, but in your bodies, minds and souls the beam, that we have turned upon you in this particular spot, is giving you a greater charge of the energies of creative force that you can carry out with you, so as to be renewed and revitalized, fortified for all that you may walk into. It is being given in many more ways than a verbal message to your ears.
