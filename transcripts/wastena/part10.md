## DISCOURSE No. 30

## Change Your Habits Now

<u>Scripture Reading</u>: John 16:17

Blessings, children of the Light. From the outer world you have come together in perfect harmony and oneness of spirit. This brings perfect accord and sustaining power that lifts your consciousness. Greater works can the Father do through you when you assemble as one, transcending the human egos.

It is over three weeks now that these sessions have been flowing through constantly, daily, and many of you have reached new heights, have had greater understanding, have come into new blessings. Some of you have entered into more of the light than others, but it is all available. Even after you have gone to your homes we shall help you to assimilate that which you have heard. It is your focus, your goal for each step that draws you up into the next. As you attain one you move on to the next. These are the phases through which your soul body is going, and you seem to comprehend more consciously, but the body does not always equalize with this light and energy that is moving through at a faster rate. At times the ego will resist moving with the soul into greater light. Then there is an imbalance, and there may be physical reactions in the form of headaches and other pains. There must be the all-out giving of the will to the Father, even the pain. Give it over unto the Father. In Him there is no pain, so pain disappears when you awaken in the spirit.

There are various physical sensations that go along with this high intensity of light energies, the higher frequency of light into which you are moving, for you are being prepared to move into the higher dimension before long. Many are going over instantly without pain and going into service in the other dimension. Part of the plan. Others will still be on Earth and will be drawn up into the clouds into celestial ships at the time the Lord gives the command. This should bring humility and gratitude unto your souls when you realize how much your Master and Lord loved you and still loves you. How he prayed for you before he left the world! He prayed that you could benefit from his knowledge of his oneness with the Father. He made that contact for you, made it to be available for you when you believed in him and his oneness. He offered you his love and lay down his life for you so that you could identify with him. Know that his prayer shall be answered, for he knew how to pray, and in his love and wisdom he made contact for you with the Father.

We have given you lessons on the begotten sons of God, sent forth to unfold and mature and come to the perfection of the plan. God will not leave this undone. He will see that it takes place. You were given free will to choose this or not. As we repeat these things over and over it goes a little deeper each time through the layers of materiality until it sinks into your souls. They are being called to come forth in new life. Hear that call and remember that physically you were born of water; the souls of you were born of Spirit--that which is eternal--and they are being prepared for re-entrance into Paradise, which is the fourth dimensional millenium of light. For the next thousand years there shall be abundant and glorious life for the citizens of the Kingdom of Heaven on Earth.

Having chosen this, you have been selected, your Lord has chosen you to fulfill the rest of his mission. Do not betray him by pretending to love and follow him and yet betray him by losing interest or focusing upon other things--becoming distracted by the superficial. Put all things away from you and go forth in serious search. Know that the indwelling spirit shall reveal to you all that shall be needed during this coming age. You have all the potentials, all the attributes of the God-nature in your being but have been functioning in the lower self, that lower nature which is not eternal. All that time you have had access to the higher nature of the Spirit of God. Use visualization, use divine imagination (it is God-given) and realize. With the spiritual eye, explore within. It is the greatest of all adventures. The greatest satisfaction is to find the God center within your holy temple.

We work with you many times during the day and in the night when the body rests, the mind is quiet and the soul takes flight, but you must also come consciously into realization. It is about time! Everything depends upon your focus every moment of the day. If you shift it often and various things distract you, the first thing you know you are back in the world again, conforming to human nature and that, of course, is always on the defensive. You can recognize this by the quick justification it attempts. In the spirit there is calm deliberation, there is remembrance of the covenant between the soul and its God, and a deep desire to follow through, to remain true to all that is conviction within the soul. Your need now is for conscious cooperation with the soul, to help it fulfill its covenant.

Many are curious but they are flighty and superficial. An inquiring mind with the right motives can be stimulated to start searching. This can be with true dedication. There is a difference. But there is indeed only a remnant, a minority of such souls. Even Christianity itself is a minority upon the whole Earth, and out of that there is only a remnant of those who are deeply serious about being about their Father's business. Many do not have the time, they say, or they are ashamed of their friends' criticism. Others do not understand and make no effort to understand. One is either traveling the way or he is not, but when he is, he keeps his eyes upon the Christ, upon the goal which is the attainment of the Christ-nature in himself, for this is God's will. Who is man to alter this or delay it. It comes in direct proportion to your dedication.

You are disciples who were with your Lord when he walked the Earth. Though you have had many incarnations since, the seed of truth that was planted within you is eternal and you recognize your Lord. You still desire to be part of the Harvest Age and are remaining true unto the end. Actually the believers remaining will be taken up into the clouds just ahead of the greatest tribulation. The preparation of those entering into the fourth dimension, the Paradise State, is rapidly going forth. You are being conditioned so that you will match the frequency of light into which you are being drawn. This is the mercy of God Who answers your prayers before you express them, because the desire of the soul to meet the purposes of God is bringing it about. In His wisdom and mercy you are given protection before you realize the need for it.

So your hearts should be full of love and thanksgiving. Many are the blessings, but many are the aches and pains caused by resistance to the Light that would draw you from out of the world. You are going through the portals into Paradise, so daily surrender your life, your will, your ego unto the Father, and He shall renew you. He shall make so many things come to life in a new way for you. The Father silently moves within the temple and power is increased. The Master told his disciples to go into the homes of those who would listen so that the Father's Light and Love could move through. Just so would he have you carry it from place to place.

The Spirit of the Living God brings forth transformations and manifestations in the lives of individuals and families. The carrier, one who knows that the Eternal Presence is within, has the authority to discharge the healing currents, the life-giving energies into each household in which God is honored. Then he moves on. This has happened many times. God works in mysterious ways; He moves silently in Spirit through the vessels who claim oneness with Him and know it as a living Presence. Every moment available you must learn to do this--to claim it, to be it--for it is the power of God which shall release all that is bound.

Begin to take your focus off things of Earth that would bind you--your possessions or other things that are such clutter in your lives and minds. You can lighten your obligations and responsibilities, can simplify your lives so as to come into the realization of God's power in you. You do not have to know <u>how</u> to do it; just claim it and give thanks unto your God. Then He shall move through you. It is a way unknown to you, for you have forgotten how Spirit functions. There are many who are not willing to give up the time or to go through the trouble.

Each of you has shown the soul desire and the willingness to come together in Spirit with those of like purpose. This would be considered foolishness by the world, but in reality it is obedience unto God. In your Lord's name you obey and follow, in his name you are claiming his nature. In that nature you rise up and show your belief by taking authority in the soul. In quietness and confidence shall be your strength. He will use it, but you practice being still long enough so that it may move through the very center of your sanctuary within. Believe this and you may take new confidence because you believe. The soul takes confidence in that which it knows is Spirit and new strength comes, for it is the strength of the Lord. When you realize that you are nothing in the human nature and put that down so that you rise up in the higher nature, then God moves through His own. We repeat so much and speak slowly and deliberately that your souls can assimilate this and come into a realization of the truth.

You have become so accustomed to repeating the words of your Scripture by rote that to you they have lost their meaning. Realize what each word and each concept is telling you. Relax, believe it, accept it without strain. Still the emotions and idle thoughts and know that God takes over in the temple offered unto Him.

In the upper room the Master lifted the consciousness of his disciples in preparation, before he went into higher planes. It is necessary also that we do this with you, so you have been lifted in consciousness increasingly. At times it would be as though you were looking at the ground from a height and become dizzy. You have had several of these experiences because you could not keep your equilibrium with such high voltage. Yet if you can recognize it for what it is and are persistent in intent, you can rise up and go on. Jesus stumbled under his cross; you will stumble under yours. The cross was symbolic of all the crossroads, all the decisions, all the testings, that you are going through. Jesus said, "Pick up thy cross and follow me." Rise up and continue. One day, as you finally forget yourself in some moment of quietness you will open and in a flash some of these realizations will come to you, consciously, and you will wonder how you missed them before.

So you are being conditioned and cared for, and all this cultivated by the Spirit in the fertile soil brings the increase, produces the fruit. You are given the opportunity to claim your identity, to claim the power. Know the Source of that power and let your motives be of the highest, for God's purposes. Let yourselves be moulded by the Great Potter and, in complete surrender to His will, you shall be ready to move into Paradise.

This is one of the refining stages before the greater calamities hit the Earth, and man is not taking time to learn or to obey. If you have learned obedience unto the Father, then in the moment of need you will obey, when you hear the instructions. The habits you form now are those that shall determine whether or not you enter into Paradise. So be careful of your habits and your thoughts and emotions. Have no resentments of the petty things that come your way; be loving, be forgiving, be obedient unto your Lord. He is so near to you. Not only has he been in your midst but he is now active in the affairs of the children of God, for he is preparing for action in the time when he shall draw unto himself those whom the Father gave him.

In your hearts you do indeed desire truth; your motives are pure. Always be sure that your desire is to glorify your God and not to boast of self. Beware of spiritual pride, for pride goeth before a fall. We use the parables and the sayings of your own language to help to convey to you the reality of God's Kingdom of etherial worlds of abundant life, and to increase your faith. Do not be deceived because there seems to be a slowness in the unfoldment. Very shortly things will happen so rapidly that you will beg for them to slow down, so use the "tarrying" time.

There will be many more earthquakes, terrible famines and much more danger of nuclear weapons. All this is building up just as surely as the hatred in the hearts of those who riot is building up for the next outburst. Evil is finally coming into full swing. When it does the great intervention shall take place and God shall move to draw the believers into ships. This will be to preserve them for the Father's purpose while the unbelievers suffer great tribulation.

Have faith and know that you shall be shown things later. You could not understand if you were told just what you may be doing in the Paradise State, or even in the time of the cataclysms. First we must help you to gain fortitude and balance. Then you will be able to withstand changes, to retain your equilibrium as the early Christian martyrs did--rejoicing in your souls because you will know that you are closer to the realization of God's Kingdom on Earth. So you are told to keep your focus high, to look upon the Master who is leading you out of the temptations of evil. Give thanks for this. You seem to walk as though nothing is happening when the mighty drama of the solar system is taking place. God's salvation is come unto man, and so few have realized it.

So many go their usual ways and care not about furthering God's message unto Earth; yet there are those of you who have been dedicated to advancing the "good news" that the Kingdom, Eternal Life, is near at hand. It is already manifesting in you, a little at a time, and at the moment the change takes place the soul will be ready. We have been feeding your souls, energizing them according to the Father's will, caring for and ministering unto His own. But it is only with your consent that guardians work with you. Follow through and obey that which you know in your heart, for the watchmen of the night shall see the dawning sun. Only a few more years and your Lord shall come in great glory in the chariots in the skies, with the Host following, in intensified light, ultimately to rule the Earth.

He has already conquered the world, as such. Know this so that you realize there is no power on Earth stronger than that of God's. The demons of darkness cannot prevail as long as you are consciously aware of the light of the creative forces of God, and you know your Master, know his voice. Be firm in following him as he leads you out. It is this that shall finally help you slip into place. With a flash of realization you shall know that you are one of the sons of God, shall know that the Father is within you in the form of the indwelling spirit. He will come into action as you give consent and get out of the way. In all your affairs let the higher nature function. It is not something that you talk about when you come together, it is a way of moving constantly in the higher nature. Then you have access to all answers, and before you call He shall answer.

You are souls who have climbed to the mountain top. Your desires, your dedication have brought you this far and realizing that it is the spirit within you that doeth the works. You will get out of the habit of thinking of yourself as one coming into goodness and doing such good works that you deserve a reward. This will no longer enter your minds. You will know that it is the Father within that doeth the works and in all thy ways acknowledge Him. You are indeed being made ready to enter into the Paradise State. You are being prepared to move right into the Father's service.

Pray for those who have gone before you. This gives them added light and strength, for the prayer of the soul sends forth light from the temple of you. God's supply never runs out. As you direct it consciously, in the Lord's name and nature, you rise up to meet that nature and know that you are one in Him, just as surely as you all have known that you were one in Spirit here in this room. The vibrations will remain here, for the power of God is strong. Daily, let your hearts be filled with thanksgiving and love. After much prayer and dedication and offering your entire being unto the Father, in your moment of weakness He shall raise you up into full strength, His strength which is eternal.

The Light is increasing in your midst; the power is great. Be filled with these energies; let the healing currents restore you to wholeness, integrated in Him. You shall be moved to help further the Father's works, for the Spirit moves through those who remain true to their posts. When a disciple does not turn away from an assignment of God, God does not turn away from him; nor does He withhold the fruits of His Spirit which multiplies, reproduces, brings forth in greater measure, as it is handed on to the trustees of His mission.

A sacred trust is left with each one of you--a sacred trust! Our Lord is in your midst.

\* \* \* \* \*

Come ye apart from the world and follow me. You are the flock moving through the door with the Shepherd: disciples of love, those that feed my sheep. Your love and faithfulness shall bring you into the Kingdom. My peace I leave with you.

\* \* \* \* \*

So be it, beloveds! There is little to say after the presence of the Lord.

<u>Become that nature and glorify your Father</u>. Selah!

## DISCOURSE No. 31

## The Soul Restored

<u>Scripture Reading</u>: First letter of Peter to the dispersed tribes.

Greetings, beloveds. It is well when you are prepared within as you have been with prayer and meditation. Now you are better able to receive the higher truths of Spirit, for your minds and emotions are stilled. When you give up your ways and your will and come before your Lord with the focus on His purpose, then you transcend all else and come as a prepared vessel, to be filled with more of God's Holy Spirit. In these last days of the Age you are coming face-to-face with many things that will surprise and shock you. You are being told this ahead of time and you are being ministered unto by the angels of Heaven. You are being guided and led through the valleys of deceit by no other than the Shepherd of the flock, the Lord who has been given authority to govern the entire world as well as other flocks. And it has taken a long time to help you to come into position, into the realization that you are, even on this very day, dealing with the presence, the power of your Lord. It should not be surprising, for it was promised by the Father, but the words have fallen on deaf ears for a long time and you had to be conditioned to take the increase of light.

The body is not accustomed to receiving as much power as you have been experiencing in your midst during these past three weeks. It is like supplying you with food for the physical body, or fuel for vehicles which carry you in transportation. The needs of the spiritual body to meet what is to take place have been mercifully provided for by the Holy Spirit of God. You have benefitted by that magnification of power here and have received some conscious benefits. But most of it you were not aware of. It must be done; the nourishment of the soul is essential. Your faith in and love for the Master Jesus, who fulfilled this plan for restoring souls to renewed life, have brought this about. The desire to be one with your Lord has tremendous power and opens the door, opens the compartments of the consciousness so that finally you come into one great, expanded awareness.

The reason that we expand on different subjects from time to time is that as you grow and open we can come back with a little more on those same subjects. One of them has been the meaning of the great sacrifice on the cross; it has been difficult for you to understand. Another was when God spoke to Adam and Eve and said, "Of the fruit of this tree you must not eat or you will surely die." He meant the distraction and the deceits of the world. Even now, if you slide back into the various aspects of the world kingdom, and your motives changing accordingly, then you are again eating of the fruits of the false systems--the dishonesty, the greed, the selfishness, the hate, the lust.

Today, the business world is coming more and more under the domination of Lucifer, and soul energies are being dissipated and diluted. They were when the children of Israel, who were told to keep the race pure, intermarried with other races. The race was to be kept pure and separate from the world for God's purposes, but not because of racial superiority. Many remained true to this; many did not. Today man is forcing integration and intermarriage among those whom God has willed to be separate peoples, with each according to the purposes in His plan. Being kind and loving to all souls, being one in spirit is entirely different from intermarriage between races, whatever the race. The energies of those begotten of the Father and sent forth in His likeness for His purpose on Earth became diluted, the spirit energy began to wane and the souls were actually dying out. Lucifer tried to deceive, tried to cover this by assuring the Adamic race that it would live, for look: reincarnation kept them going. But it did not give them the lifting power for a resurrection.

Finally, when the Lord came unto man, all the way into the lowest vibration, into the world to show how to rise up out of it, he did not remain long. He was with you only thirty-three years, each year a great example of the law being fulfilled in the model--a demonstration unto you. Daily he showed, by his words, his actions, his love, that the Father was working in him. In love he lay down his life for those the Father had given unto him--the Adamic race, begotten of the Father for special purposes. The power, the energies of the Heavens were in his blood that was spilled upon the Earth--a contact of Spirit that began to renew the Earth immediately... vital, live forces of creative power, energy of God, the Creator. Thus he restored life to the dying souls.

The body had to be broken; the heart had to be broken, as it were, that in the disillusionment of the physical world and the meeting of betrayal, he could rise up in spirit. He gave man hope that that which was broken could be made whole, resurrected by the regeneration within each. And his great love and sacrifice made it possible for all to follow, through that door, that way. Many teachers of truth and light have been in the world, but this one was the first Son of God to overcome the entire Earth, to transcend it and be resurrected. This opened the door wide for the believers of God to do the same, and the dying souls were given new life and love--a regeneration--and saved from losing the individual soul awareness of oneness with the Father.

So began the restoration of those souls into balance, into purpose, into fullness of life. God has been working through this, waiting patiently for those to come forth. No more creation--the day of rest in the sense that it is the passive time when there is not the begetting. He is waiting for those whom He sent forth to come back to Him fulfilled, mission accomplished, their Christhood achieved. Mankind, working through layers and layers of materiality and deceit, with so many karmic debts upon his shoulders, could not have risen if left to himself, for there were not enough of the Divine Energies left him, due to the dilution and his unbelief. There was no longer power in their coming together under the law of Oneness.

And so in a cave, unspoiled and apart from man's motives and greeds, the Child was born. And God's power was established upon Earth once more. One came with the capacity and love, and assumed a role for a time. He carried the burden of all karmic debt upon his shoulders, so that man could rise and be free. This did not relieve man of the responsibility of working out the remainder that he was left to cope with in good faith and an attitude of love, but he was no longer trapped. He was given the key; he was shown how it could be used, how divine love could give him the power to transcend the remainder and go through unto the resurrection. But he had to believe it, identify with that law-in-action and move with it. If he claimed it and gave thanks, he was forgiven and strengthened, cleansed to a point and it would continue. As he became aware he was told to forgive himself. Once he knew not; now he knows in an entirely different perspective. Now he believes in the One the Father sent to help man, to help him become integrated once more with the spiritual powers and the nature in which he was sent forth, to help him become disentangled from the webs of the world kingdom. And finally, the entire Earth would be cleansed.

The "good news" went forth: man no longer had to go through the deceit of death and enter into the lower realms, then come back in other incarnations and try again, with no spiritual power. Now all had been changed. The doors to the lower regions were opened. The Master, during the three days, went unto Beelzebub's realm, took light to the very center of the Earth, and the door was opened for all who desired light and would follow him, going forth with him into the Paradise State. And all those who had died since Adam were brought forth. Even some who had not been of the Adamic race, when they saw, realized and desired to follow the light, and they were brought in through adoption. The Master included all who desired to follow him, and the doors of Paradise were opened. Thereafter, all who died the physical death would enter the various planes of the fourth dimension; they were no longer bound to the prison state. Some were suspended for a time until they could go further in the light. But those who believed entered into Paradise with him. Adam had returned to Paradise.

Those to come after would then go to Paradise. Many times you did this between incarnations and you would receive greater strength. Then you would come back with new insight and new choices as to how to enter and to what environment. This would be to help the soul work toward full restoration. It is as though you would take an infant with malnutrition and bring it back to life through supplemental feeding, adding all that in which he was deficient. Then he could move forward and progress. So it is with the soul bodies. Now you are growing because greater strength in you is lifting you further and further. Once you learn that the very things which have distracted and held you prisoner are the things upon which you have focused and fed, which you have desired on Earth, then many of these shall begin to fall away from you. You no longer have appetite for the things of the world's system. Gradually, they shall all fall away and you shall truly rejoice in the spiritual realms of the Father.

Come face to face with the realization of what is happening now, and understand how mercifully God has cared for you throughout your days of blindness. Now that you believe yourselves to be awakened, do not sit back complacently but desire to expand. Be willing to let go the old; cling not to the things of Earth or even memories of the past. Go forth in expectancy and new light, for your Father is leading you home through the Shepherd who became the visible demonstration of the law in action and gave hope unto you. In faith, go through the trials and testings in the years ahead. Sometimes you begin to feel that it has been so long, maybe it will never happen. Yet it will happen; it is already happening and is bringing a change in you.

Then, you begin to walk in reality. Your feet are yet on the surface of the Earth, but your focus is on the Master who overcame and glorified the Father. His example should inspire you and help you not to be distracted by the outer world. You are still confused in this, no matter how well meaning you are.

His instructions are now coming back to you, as soul remembrance is tapped. When you hear the instructions anew in the outer consciousness, as the Lord calls his own, you know. You open up and respond because you know in your heart the instructions were for you, are still for you. You are now moving into action--slowly at the moment, but standing at attention until your Lord makes ready the way. He who goes before you will not forget you but will draw you out of the world of temptation, out of the valley of the shadow of death--that deceit of temporal life and the pattern of birth and rebirth. He shall draw you into the fullness of the Father's Life in the higher realms.

As you respond in your hearts to these messages, it is the Spirit working. No one is convincing your minds of anything; no one desires to. It is matching the need, bringing into awareness the affinity between those that are born of the Spirit, who have walked together throughout the ages. Many who incarnate together find an immediate rapport, and nothing can hinder this. No man cometh except the Father draw him; the Father within the temple of you draws you unto those to whom you respond, for those of the Spirit recognize Spirit in all who are moving together in the same purpose.

Receive the same food, work together for the same purpose, and slowly remembrance comes to the surface of the waters. Then you feel as though you have known each other always. This is so, for in Spirit, there is no past, no present or future; no distance or time. Those born of the Spirit know those of Spirit and all are coming into oneness in the Father's plan. So learn to live each day with a greater realization that your soul is being restored to its vital energies of life, to its eternal life awareness--that God's love is increasing in you. You are drawn together to work hand-in-hand with those who follow the Shepherd, keeping your eyes on the one going before. Then know that each day you are receiving your daily bread--for the soul--receiving energies when realization comes of contact with the indwelling Spirit. The energies flow through. You will also have your needs met on the physical plane. It is simultaneous. You are functioning in the outer physical and yet you are living with God in the inner life of the temple. This brings a different kind of life, a different perspective.

In your individual life you are doing this daily. Part of the time you are aware of the indwelling Presence of God, aware of the Spirit functioning in you. Part of the time you believe that you are begotten of the Divine Nature and are supposed to take authority in that nature. You turn to the inner awareness part of the time, and yet it <u>is</u> only part of the time. <u>All</u> difficulties can be solved by turning within. When two or more are gathered together in His Name and Nature--in that Higher Nature, that consciousness, that realization of the Son, One with the Father--then things happen. Spirit is magnified in your midst. But you cannot hold this and you turn again to the outer. When you turn inside-out, as it were, there will be abundant strength and that within shall manifest outwardly and help man.

Take to yourselves the instructions to the early believers. They are of Spirit. Believe that every word the Master left is for you. Be ye like unto him. Learn to be at home in the inner life, to be at ease in the Spirit, by practising just as you are doing here. Each day power has built in your midst because there was deliberate assembling together in the Lord's Name. Purpose, never lose sight of the purpose. And as you return unto your homes, sit or lie down quietly or in any other way that is comfortable enough to put the physical body at ease. Then it will not distract you from entering the house of your Lord. Enter, explore within and ye shall find food from the storehouse--knowledge, understanding--revelation. In the Light, rising in the love you shall learn to see in the fourth dimension. Cultivate those habits which are constructive and sit and wait upon your Lord. Do not fret if you do not seem to have a tangible answer. When you do have an idea, or a prompting that seems born of Spirit, then follow through, no matter what the cost.

When you return home, if you do not have a partner in prayer, do not rush out to find one humanly. Turn within and pray that the Father draw one to you that will enter into the oneness of Spirit with you. Then be alert and read the signs as they develop, as various ones come near. Two or more in the Divine Nature of Spirit can come together in oneness just as all of you now are in a circle of oneness. You can feel and experience the power moving through, and greater action will follow according to the laws of God.
