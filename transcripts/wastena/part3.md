You may be reassured that you are part of the Kingdom; but He can use you more effectively when he has perfected the being. He brings the being closer toward that perfected state or finished product that the seed was sent forth to be when you are out of the way. The most likely times are when you are asleep, when you are unconscious, or when you have deliberately chosen to be quiet, be still and love your God. It is more powerful than all the words.

Be thankful that you, the son, have the connection with the Father. Be still and He will do the works through you. Be still and you will know more and more often. Feel the power of the Presence in and around you and give thanks for it and rejoice for it. Do not think in terms of just having a psychic experience. God is with you. You are made in His nature. It must come to the finished product. And He will do it when you are out of the way in your busy, busy human nature. If you have affairs to bring into order, certain obligations, of course you must finish until the time when you no longer need them. But you can hurry to finish so that you can then be still.

One day will come the time when you will no longer need to pick it up again. You will have an immortal vacation from humanity, leaving the mortal behind as well as the human nature and the false creation that Lucifer brought forth in a dream in projection, according to his motives. Of course, this brought the twist and distortion over all that God had created. God created, but Lucifer produced chaos through deception, and you became the victim of the chaos. Rejoice in the day that you will not have to return to the chaos, but will establish order out of it. <u>This</u> is progress, not what man has called progress.

Nothing is wasted. Atoms of energy in chaotic knots are turned into God's glory by re-establishing order. You learn to control the atoms of divine energy and all the molecular motion that is taking place. You bring the atoms into order and convert them into other purposes so there not be atoms at random nor brought together as you would find entanglement, but smoothed out. Peace upon the waters. Be still and know. To know is to realize and be in contact with God-power from the beginning of creation to the reforming, the converting, the transforming action into the perfected state of His plan--moving in line as the tide.

The law of knowing is the law with the wisdom and the intelligence of God in you; knowing the right timing, knowing when and where and how, without struggle; quietly knowing, bringing all into order, and governing it in justice and love and mercy and wisdom, with God's intelligence in action through you. It is only the human that has to ask "How do I find out?" Obey; commune within until the connection is complete, with the Father and the son in perfect alignment, because the son has released his own desires. He has stopped holding on to anything that would hold him to the self-centered nature, and then God moves through in perfect serenity.

But man has tried to bring it about by his own struggles, when he consciously claimed this decision. You can keep yourself in line by learning to control what you do, but you can not force. The God-power there is released in you when you step out of the way in the humaness. You do not have to beg God to do something, but rather be direct in your command to yourself that you get out of the way; be still. God already knows. His knowing will move through you. The desire of your hearts is for this. You are being lifted up into it. It will be accomplished, but you may grow weary while it is happening because you vacillate back and forth, and because you have not yet taken authority completely in the soul of you. Take authority; desire to live only in the Christ consciousness in everything you do about your daily affairs--when you speak to one another, when you walk, when you accomplish some project.

We certainly are aware that it is difficult when you go among those of the world who have no concern for the kingdom coming. When they do not have concern for the kingdom coming in, they will of necessity be ruled out, for it is those that choose to manifest as this in God's Kingdom that He will use. All have had the opportunity of knowing at one time or another of this choice, and they make the choice. But the promptings in you--even as you were small, or even as you would begin to read, or be drawn toward someone--feel something that you now tie in as part of this. God in His mercy was drawing you up into remembrance.

Pray for more remembrance. It will come as you identify with the early Christians, from the time Jesus spoke to them to the time the apostles spoke to them--spoke to you. Put yourself in that place, for you were there. The early Christians are still the same Christians, having travelled a long journey. You have been promised the arrival. You have been promised that the Lord is your shepherd and will not leave you until you have entered in. He went in his greater power that he could assist you throughout this time, but he promised to return in the form that he left. And as your soul is cleansed more and more, the holy temple--the body--shall be cleansed and all brought into balance.

Therefore you must keep your wits about you as you go through these last days so that you avoid deception from the psychic side, from the world side, and from that self in you that would be glad to deceive you. These keep you alert. And watch; govern your thoughts, learn to control your habits, your eating, and all that you do. Take control and do what you believe to be wisest and ask that God give you the strength to do it. Be careful that you are not influenced too much by others. Now, this is difficult because many fine souls in this day are spreading a good influence and lifting--and, of course, these you join in with; but there are others that have gotten out of balance, gone off on a tangent, taking only a part of that which is good and building around it instead of using it as a part of the whole, just as the churches have come under much of man's organization and creed and doctrine and rules. Watch for these. Man has divided more and more; so have many of these groups of this day--the "New Age" as they have been called. Many are waiting for a change; some on a physical level, some on a psychic level, and some on a soul level.

The cosmic level is drawing the soul up into the very beauties of the heavenly state, and those that have, as you would call it in your vernacular, gone overboard in certain areas, become fanatical. This is not as it was meant to be, but one can learn, experience, and acquire a certain amount of balance anyway, and with that prayer of discernment, know when to speak out, when to be quiet, whom to speak to, and to speak only on those things which would avoid a great deal of confusion. Many rush out, wanting to have experiences like their neighbors. They are thrilled over the sensations or psychic phenomena they hear about. They want to see, they want to have, want to experience. Self, self, self.

The heart, of course, can desire to open up and see whatever God would reveal, but there again is the testing of the motives. Why? What are they looking for? One person shouldn't judge another, but each one should ask himself what the motive is behind it. Is it the seeking of pleasure, the seeking of excitement? There are many that love to follow the fire; it is exciting. There are others that stay home and pray without the excitement.
