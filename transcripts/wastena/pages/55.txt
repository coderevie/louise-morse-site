Just as surely as you say, "Yes, I will take that assignment,"
say "Yes, I choose," and command in the name of the Christ who made it so, that
the soul take over and manifest in the body, its temple. Take command. Then
the faith shall be increased. Then you shall know that it has more power than
the human nature and act as though you know it. This takes practice, or you
will slide back into the dream.

Consciously and alertly remember and be aware of the soul of you
which is the I AM that would pray unto God--the I AM desiring. And the prayer
really is the sincere desire of the soul to be in union with that from which it
has apparently been separated. It was sent forth; it was projected for the
magnification of the Spirit. The forgetfulness was the barrier. If one could
drug you until you forgot who your family was and where your home was, you would
wander and stray with no purpose, no ambition, no goal, no desire to see anyone.
And think of the joy when the drug would wear off. Your memory would return.
"Oh, where is my loved one? I must go." The desire immediately draws you to-
gether, but not until you remember. Then you choose according to your desire.
But you cannot even choose if you do not remember.

God brings remembrance unto all. You are gradually remembering.
You are realizing your identity. You are not quite believing it all the way.
You want to believe it; but there must be the connecting link between the soul
awareness and the outer consciousness, and that breakthrough makes them as one.
Your faith shall make you whole; your faith shall bring back the memories. Do
not be impatient and expect it the same hour. Two thousand years ago Jesus
told the disciples that the Kingdom of Heaven was at hand and commissioned Paul
to speak to these, as was read by the channel. While you are waiting for the
return of the Lord for whom you watched for two thousand years, be patient. Be
faithful whether it be two thousand years, thirty years, or ten years. It is
far less now than two thousand years.

Thus, before the cataclysms that shall bring panic to many and
death to many--at which time you cannot be in silence--the mercies of God have
given you this time now; not only these three weeks, but whatever time there is.
And not even angels in heaven know the exact moment of that breakthrough. It
has been given that a millennium will begin by the year 2000 or 2001--in that
early time. But the divine intervention has already begun. It will not come
in visible mass until a crucial moment, but that is the meaning of the ships in
your sky. The warring is still going on between Lucifer and his hosts and the
hosts sent by the Lord of Earth. There are those protecting and counteracting
much of the damage of Earth, and helping the faithful remnant to prepare in
this time.

It is not a coasting time; neither is it one in which one must
run hither and yon and be actively busy in the physical sense. It is a time to
get busy inside of you. Prepare inside of you. Do not stock up all your money,
all your food, and look for a safe corner. Turn within where the only refuge
is--the solid rock upon which you shall stand, the realization, and the practice
of this realization. The Christ is one with the Father. Jesus overcame the
earth and shall lead each and all that move in this Christ body. The Christ in
you must be brought forth before the cataclysms. Do not wait for earthquakes
and tidal waves and then say, "Lord, prepare me." This is the time of the pre-
paration within. Of course, much has gone before, but the greater is now--not
only the cleansing, the purifying, and the asking for greater faith, but the
asking for the remembrance. Now you are at least hoping and praying that you
have the balance to take it. Seek equilibrium, inner poise, and balance in all
things that you may be able to balance that which the Lord shall reveal unto
you, and not be pushed out of balance by opening to more than you can handle.
