the time as being a phase you are moving through toward something else. But it

Self complacency, however, will cut off the freedom of expansion
and growth. There must be a pioneer spirit within one to explore, to go farther
into that unknown to them for the moment, for their memory is cut off. You would
not think it desirable that one with amnesia continue to roam the streets, un-
aware of his identity, not knowing his home--the place that would be his true
security; having no purpose, nothing but that suspended moment. Might he not even
find it easy enough to be content that way? But would this be desirable? Would
this be development and progress? Rather you would rejoice when he began to
remember who he was, where his home was and move into his true place in the plan
of things.

Far greater then is the Divine Plan--the identity of the lost sons
of God who could not remember who they were, from whence they came or where they
were going; who were suspended in a purposeless, artificial life--getting
nowhere--having to die and come back to go through the same things over and over,
lifetime after lifetime, until finally the light began to dawn as the conscious-
ness began to expand. Some began to remember; others were reminded. Deep in the
inner being, memory began to come to the surface of the waters of the conscious-
ness.

Some are only now realizing what the Master said to them nearly
2000 years ago. Slow development but sure; arrested development, blindness due
to choice (the law of cause and effect), but the blindness would not have come
had they not disobeyed God's laws and pulled away from His will. If you touch
fire it will burn you. If you have been warned and told not to, but you chose to
do it, then you have brought it on yourself. Yet the pain will be just as great,
and it will not be God's doing; it will be your responsibility because of your
choice, your disobedience.

This is the price you are now paying, the price of disobedience.
Realize that there has been this deviation from the perfect plan, and repent.
God, in His intelligence and wisdom, knew of this possibility perhaps even the
probability, but at the same time He allowed for it. He allowed for the dis-
pensation of mercy to extend even unto the disobedient; He offered man a chance
if he would accept the great, loving sacrifice of the Master which had to be
greater than the disobedience of the sons. You were allowed a way in through
forgiveness, and if you took this way you were to progress and come back into
obedience. This may not be just as you desired, but you could never have met
your karmic debts, they had piled so high. Now you put yourself in a position
far worse than before if you disobey after accepting the great gift of mercy and
forgiveness.

And so the consequences were modified; the blow was softened. In
accepting the gift you also accepted the requirement of obedience. It would be
far better had you stayed outside if you did not intend to obey. But obedience
must not be to some dogma or doctrine or creed of man, but to your God and to
that which He gave you in your hearts and in the preserved "Writings" that the
Master gave you in parable form. Read them more often. Identify with them. Read
them with the soul, not the intellect. Step into line more and more by desiring,
by obeying the commandments. Love thy God first of all, above all else, with all
thy being; and then love thy neighbor as thyself. In love you expand. If you
