The time is now, even as it was when Jesus gave this same message.
It is even more so because the larger Kingdom is near at hand. It can come in
each one individually, but the Lord shall rule the Earth in his glorified body
when those that are ready for the fourth dimension are gathered in. Man has
misunderstood. He thought of living in the Kingdom as walking with his head in
the clouds, unable to cope with things of Earth. This is not the case. One
uses higher intelligence which brings greater patience, greater insight, greater
control, authority to use the potentials of the divine nature for God's purposes.
He is going toward a day of freedom. Know the truth and the truth shall set you
free. The truth is in the understanding of the laws of God within the kingdom
of you. God will reveal those as you turn to Him, more and more. Some spend
five minutes of the day with God and demand immediate answers. Others have gone
for years, quietly communing, gratefully receiving whatever glimpse they are
shown. And they realize that their prayers are answered, that they are receiving
much guidance and protection. Guardian angels are walking with them (as the law
provides) and yet are not taking responsibility and doing all things for them.

Man must learn to take responsibility, evaluate carefully, and make
decisions, but it must be done in his higher nature. Consciously practice, with
levers that would help remind you. Think of your consciousness as an elevator,
going up and down, and if you feel that you are in a lower state of consciousness
than you should be, push the button, deliberately choose. Say, "Lord, lift me
up onto higher ground." And you will be, through your very desire. The Lord is
there; the power is in you, lifting when you turn your thought unto God and to
His plan as exemplified by the Christ in the form of the Master Jesus.

Lift up your eyes unto the hills, and know that your redemption
draweth nigh. See the signs in the sky, the many signs, including celestial ships
coming to your planet. There has been plenty of proof of them whether you have
seen them or not. Massive sightings or landings have been delayed by God's
wisdom. The time is not right but yet not far off. There are other signs:
comets, asteroids, even the satellites. The earth, which has for a long time
been out of its original orbit, shall move back into place. But most essential
of all is that you re-orient around the true Center, and let your consciousness
and your focus be upon the Christ who is one with the Father. Desire that the
Father's will be accomplished in Earth and in you, and do not complain when it
takes place. This is the only way back through the kingdom of darkness, the
state of deception.

Souls have been sleeping, and the Master came to touch Earth, to
make contact with the souls, to restore them to life. It is the story of the
"Sleeping Beauty" in your children's fairy tales. It was preserved for the souls
of the children. Their mothers would read the story to them and it would enter
their souls, bringing to remembrance the love of the Christ who made contact with
the sleeping bride (God's true church) being made ready for the bridegroom. And
that union shall bring the Kingdom into full function again, as though the past
time had never been. Many times we have made reference to the stories preserved
for your children; they are pearls of great price, buried and preserved in
parables, that they be available when the souls that had "eyes to see and ears to
hear" could pick them up and move forward. Many times in the past everything
