These are the parents to whom one will say, "Well done." The Lord who raised
them up unto His purposes shall give them recognition. There should be more of
these family units brought up in God's ways, to enter into the kingdom together,
to be in that Kingdom of Spirit so that they need not return to the wheel of
birth and rebirth after the millennium. Move forward. You have earned a great
deal, though it is God's grace that gives you the bounty.

These are difficult days at this end of the age, but wise are the
young people who will not be disturbed by others of the crowd who go their ways
to chaos. They will have faith and look to a higher goal. We would tell them to
go forward in the Lord. He has raised you through many incarnations to be a
worthy watcher for his coming unto Earth. Walk in the ways of thy Lord and walk
into Eternal Life. Give thanks unto God that He provided you with parents of
faith and spiritual direction, for through that guidance you have found your way,
and the Lord shall work wonders through you.

There are many of you young people, in this day, with great en-
lightenment and spiritual awareness within. You respond and the spirit within
bears witness to the truth, whether or not your minds fully understand. The
Spirit draws you to be used to best opportunity in God's coming Kingdom; you are
being raised up for this purpose.

Be faithful unto the end, be true to your goal, let love expand in
you, and the consciousness that God gave you shall expand and expand until all
is light. Much shall be brought to your soul remembrance, much shall come in
through light, and you shall know that your God liveth, liveth in you. You are
God-life coming forth to rebuild the Earth, to make new all things upon it. We
add our blessings, that you young people especially (in whatever land) rise up
into new understanding. Many of you can go farther than those of other genera-
tions, for you have entered with readiness to obey. If you have learned
obedience as children, then as children of God you know how to obey Him. All
shall work together for good to glorify His name.

We bless you and give you strength and courage to take advantage
of the wonderful opportunities of this day of your Lord. Much is coming to pass.
You shall be a great part of this adventure of spiritual life and cosmic change,
as old things are put down for the new and greater life.

Your Lord shall work with you, and you shall know him. He has been
with you much in this Retreat. Give thanks unto God that you are coming into the
realization of your true Shepherd, your Lord of Earth. Be one in his conscious-
ness, that you become One with the Father, realizing it to the fullest.

May you go in peace and blessing, for you are coming into the beam
of greater Light, and closer to the will of the Father as your desires draw you,
through love. As you give consent you are putting down the human will and
rising up in God's will. Divine nature is beginning to manifest by degrees. Be
ever aware of this within; stand in the true faith that God is reality in you.
Bless you. He is with you. We all humbly bow to our Lord of Earth. Selah.

So be it! The Shepherd knows his flock. Be ye one with him. Know
your Shepherd. How near he is unto you many times and you know him not. But
this week you have seen him, have known him. Remain in this consciousness of
oneness with him. It is this identification which brings all things to memory.
You shall be raised up to be like him. Selah.
