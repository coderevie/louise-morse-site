The most difficult part for you is to be patient, to be still, to
be meek, putting down the human will, and yet rising up and taking authority in
the higher will. We realize this is confusing to you. It is hard for you to
know the difference between them. Pray for more help with this. For that is the
desire of your hearts. The desire shall bring the wisdom to discern. You shall
know the truth and the truth shall set you free. Even if you do not see it,
believe it, for your Lord went before you as an example and he gave unto you the
assurance that this would take place in you.

We repeat slowly, for we are sending this concept in Light into your
being, riding upon the wave of the vibration of the Word. But while we are wait-
ing for the Word to sink in we are penetrating your cells of intelligence and
life, "bombarding" them with concepts of reality, hoping that one day you will
see--an illumination. We are letting this sink into your minds and yet we are
sending forth the light vibrations, the truth of the concept, that it sink into
your souls. So we are feeding you in two ways. So you trust us and offer us the
opportunity, even though you do not understand just how this is done. You have
not the comprehension for all these details, so it is better that you do not
know all now. The more you know consciously the more you become more confused
until the time comes that you can know in the fullness.

And so we work with you as you open to receive and accept, for that
which we bring is first of the Lord, and we channel it through by fusing with the
consciousness of the soul of our channel. The waves and vibrations of sound
come through the voice box and enter by impulses into your mind. The strengthen-
ing of the soul-body is taking place, and concepts are moving into it. This
light brings you greater understanding to the soul and it is rising to the surface
of the waters of the consciousness, breaking through. Then you say, "I remember!
Oh yes, I understand this." A new light dawns. And you have had another break-
through of light. You are truly going up the steps. The farther you go the
greater the need for prayer. You are as little children, growing, moving through
each phase toward maturity. Angels were giving the soul milk for a time; then
the fruits of the spirit began to appear. Now your souls are receiving the meat
of the body of the composite Christ, that of his nature, the very light energies
of creative force--God power. This is the "body" that the Master spoke of. You
must partake of that. He has expanded in consciousness and drawn you unto him-
self; now you must feed on that nature, on that energy. Be strong in your con-
victions and the Spirit of God within you shall bring it about. Remember: you
desire, you give over self, you lay down your will; seek first the Kingdom of God
and the Father shall do the works through you. And you shall be raised up to
perfect fulfillment, you shall be the finished product--that of the Christ nature
receiving its crown of glory. In other words, the projected or the begotten, sent
forth of the Father, comes into its fullness, expressing the Father's will, the
Father doing the works through this union of consciousness. Surrender and unite
with Him. Accept that which the Father does through you.

Master, to realize that within, to prepare for the Kingdom. Even as the Master
