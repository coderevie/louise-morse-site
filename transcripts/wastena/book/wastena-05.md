Scripture Reading: First letter of Peter to the early churches.

Our greetings to you again; we are constantly with you. We desired that the message from your Holy Writings be read to you tonight, for we would direct you to that which God has preserved as instructions for you for this day. You are the same followers of the Christ with the same problems, the same human natures--the same souls being lifted up out of that human nature. So the letters that were brought through the early apostles were God-given. Thus it is brought to your remembrance as it is directed again unto your souls, calling you together once more, calling you to remember that which you have been, are, and will be.

Every sentence that was read could just as well have been said by us directly to your souls, for this message is the one that had already been given by your Lord, directed through the early ones who in this Age are now fulfilling some of the same purposes. So it is like going back in your early family life, identifying with certain of the early days and relationships. You become attuned again. You pick up where you left off in consciousness. It is drawing all that into the consciousness of the third dimension today, all your experiences, needs and purposes, bringing them through to the surface. Time is being telescoped. This message is just as real as it would be if the Lord had delivered it through Peter today, as real now as then.

There is new power added when it is read anew to you and moving through in what you might call electrical impulses, the spiritual impulses of light moving into the very cells of your being through the voice vibration, through the content of the message. Light is penetrating and entering your soul bodies, as though you were being fed by injection. But you are being fed by projection. It becomes substance in you as you are raised in consciousness, as you are led point by point, and as we do the best that we can to raise you up into the constancy of the higher focus where the Lord leads us all. Come under that guidanod of His pervading spirit.

You are receiving in many more ways than you realize as you sit quietly in this prepared atmosphere and setting, not only in this beautiful place of nature, not only in the circle of warm-hearted and loving friends, souls open= ing to receive with you, but in your bodies, minds and souls the beam, that we have turned upon you in this particular spot, is giving you a greater charge of the energies of creative force that you can carry out with you, so as to be renewed and revitalized, fortified for all that you may walk into. It is boing given in many more ways than a verbal message to your ears.

Expect this and move into it, trusting in your Lord. Each one becomes a vessel through which this enters and passes through, and the collective vessel, the group consciousness, the group heart opens up as this is allowed, Then comes a greater capacity to receive; in multiples it is increased for those souls who can love one another and come together as one, becoming oxpanded vessels, the Holy Grail into which the Father may pour the living waters of life. Thus you become renewed. You are used in many ways that you have not yot recognized, as you allow the Spirit to use you and trust the purposes of your Lord and all that work together for good in His namo.

Many take divergent paths and go into tangents, seeking the sense~ tional. They are often content with phenomena, and may become suspended in that limitation, But those who will open to their Lord will be used in various ways to perform His functions on earth. This will. not be to gratify the human self.

Souls shall marvel at many things that shall take place, even as in the early days of the first followers of the Christ. It was not meant to be the following of a man, but that Jesus became the one to go all the way in manifesting and demonstrating this law of the Creator Who sent forth of Himself begotten sons to extend and expand His intelligence, wisdom and love throughout the idea and plan of His creation. It is to spread Himself, you might say, out into the outermost parts so that all His creation be one with Him. Through His sons, created in His image for that purpose, it would be the far-reaching arm of the Creator in every area of His creations A most perfect plan, and those willing to be a part of this plan to be used of Him in this service--His synchronized plan of action and expression of God-power, sustained in the Creator and by Him,

There is, indeed, great purpose in life. Life is self-sustaining in that it is God--God sustaining His creation through His greater expansion and constancy of expression. So do not belittle the purpose of the holy one in the holy temple of you--the divine you. Walk in humility, knowing that the human self is nothing; step out of the way and let that divine consciousness expand into the fullness of its creative poworse You do not belong to yourself alone. Marvel at the God-expression in you, through yous without it you would not know life.

This is a new concept that you are entering into for you have always thought of yourself as just a being on earth with God manipulating from his throne on high, but it is the God within that makes you truly you. The beauty of the individuality in each soul is the varied, never-ending expression of God-power. Those with grateful hearts shall be raised into the fullest expression of this; their heritage can be nothing but joyful. The full realization of this is worth any pain you may have to suffer to attain it, even physical death. You live for Him, for He lives in you.

We repeat so much because we are trying to help you absorb it so that it become full reality within you. Thoughts that you accept as beliofs will be only momentary unless you heve a breakthrough and attain the full realization. So we cannot let you alone until this is accomplished or our mission would be unfinished. We bear with you, we minister unto you until the living reality expresses itself in fullness in you. You are being prepared to open into the great flower of the love of God, and this will be soon. The seed was planted long ago--the conception took place--then the nurturing, the caring for. Soon it will be time for the true birth into reality. Instead of being reborn again in another body to continue your efforts, you are being reborn in spirit and shall emerge in the freedom of the soul. No, you do not know how to do it consciously, in the physical, so trust the Lord who created you. He is the one with the plan; He does not fail.

You have given your consent enough to keep you in line with His purpose; you will not turn your back now. Thus you will go through tho portals. Je have been leading you there, with the great Shepherd ahead of us all. You will enter in. We cannot tell you all, but then how much is told the babe as it Zoos through the channel of birth and delivery? This that seems like a passive stage now is but the great stage of expectancy, close to the moment of delivery. This is far greater than the delivery of Israel from the bondange of Egypt. Even

with that delivery, was there not a timo of waiting and of preparation, in the wilderness, before entering that promised? And how many wearied of that waiting? Yes, forty years is as a moment in God's timing.

You have passed through part of the years; there are a few left. You are active in one sense, but you also seem to be halted as though ready to go farther, and it seems you cannot. In due season, and the season is approaching, delivery is at hand and the waiting is finished. So we encourage your faith, encourage your hope, for it is genuine. Never doubt it; do not think that you have hoped in vain. The closer you come to the moment the greater may be your impatience, but wait upon the Lord, trust that He is caring for every detail of the great emergencye Acquiesce as the babe in the cradle.’ Patience is indeed trust. You are in the arms of your Father. What better place to wait and trust.

You have been told to come apart from the world and its systems all that you possibly can, that you be not so alarmed when the systems fall. You were told, long before, to be prepared, that there would be wars and rumors of wars, fallings and crumblings,. All that which is not to be of the heavenly kingdom must quickly go to make way for the new. Do you not think that Noah had great compassion for all those on the other side of the door when the door of the ark was closed? But it was not for him to question God, nor to defiantly run out and stay with the others. God was working through a plen that would also care for the others in due seasons Noah obeyed, went apart from it all even though it might have seemed cruel to go off and leave the others. It was to obey God or not to obey. You are at this point and will be increasingly so.

Your Father knows bests; it is not for you to reason nor question Him as to why this and why that which does not seem just. He is justices; you know not yet of it. It is all relative to your particular position now. God is looking at the entire creation--past, present, future--all one great idea of God-expansion, one great plan in which He cares for all. His awareness encompasses all things, for if the only awareness in life, in each speck of any living thing is God or it would not be life, then He is there and knows what surrounds that particular expression of Him, far beyond your knowledge.

As we bring these things to your remembrance, we are leading you through the portals into greater enlightenment where suddenly you shall be illumined, lifted, transformed--no longer limited as is the caterpillar, but full of the glorious freedom and flight of the butterfly. The soul shall indeed take wings, for it shall not be limited and bound; the prison doors shall be unlocked. Knowing this, keeping this uppermost in your consciousness, you shall not be nearly so affected by the outer happeningse What is it to you if the outer world changes according to His plan? You be about your Father's business.

Pray for the uplifting of the many souls who go into the next dimension ahead of you, in many of these disasters. Your curiosity does not have to be satisfied as to why this would be. Leave that unto the Father. He cares for them in every dimension, but you can add love and light and assist because He cares through you when you, in wisdom, know how to assist. You would not save their bodies and human natures, but would, in love, assist the lifting of their souls from this. There are many reasons why some would go through the doors of death--so-called "death"--even the last time, but the soul is not dying. That would be true death.

Many shall pass over into light, and there are various ways to enter into light, just as there are various ways that you might reach a destination on Earth. You may travel by automobile, or fly in aeroplanes, or you may sit at home and dream of being in a particular area and project yourself. It is His business and remains so unto the end. You prepare in the heart so that you may go either way and enter into the fullness, Those who are not ready for this at this time will be lovingly cared for, suspended so that they not drop further, that they be ready to begin again and have every opportunity just as you have had.

The Father's Day may seem to be long to the finite mind, but Eternity provides opportunity enough for all. Eternal Life does not have to go by the clock; it goes by readiness for change, and the balancing factors that bring new opportunities. Balance of creative power is essential--balance of body, mind and soul. Spirit is energy in perfect balance. Until all things come into balance they function in various dimensions, various planes and spheres (a place for all, even imbalance), but eternal reality in perfect balance is Spirit in its fullness,

You are now the graduating class of Earth, after experiencing each age of trial and error, struggling through, floundering through. Earth became the schoolroom of trial and error--school extended until each class could graduate in due season--none being left out. You are among the remnant, the "elect of God" that is to graduate in spirit from His house of Israel into the house of the Lord.

Perhaps you look over the earth in nostalgia, and you may even have some of your incarnations come to remembrance. This may bring you sadness, and yet when you come closer to the great picture, the drama of life, you are ready to leave the days of infancy behind you, the days of childhood, the days of youth, even the days of so-called "maturity" on earth, to enter into the full maturity of spirit. Give thanks that it may be so in spite of the shortcomings.

As you enter into greater light and your consciousness expands, your shortcomings become more apparent by contrast, but the right desires and motives have brought you to a point of refinement, and the motives of the heart with willingness to expand and trust your Creator then allow the work to be finished. It is the potter that molds. the clays you become clay in His great hands, for you relinquish your right to rule as humans. You put down your will voluntarily, you put down the life of the hwnan self that you might have Life in its fullest sense, according to His plan. Thus, as you surrender you stop rebelling, acquiesce unto His plan and move into perfect alignment.

This is what is about to take place because in your hearts, your motives, desires and choice are all favorable. He knows how to finish the product, bring into perfection His sons whom He sent forth and who in maturity are now returning unto Him. Thus is He glorified--His work, His creation. No matter how far cut the son may move, he may at any time return willingly unto the Father. Even though he knows not how and he stumbles, the Father moves out to meet him in joy and thanksgiving, indescribable.

Be of joyful hearts in spite of the crumbling world. That which crumbles could not be sustained eternally, so surrender it all to His will, Let it crumble and be thankful that the new heavenly estate shall take its place, Then your willing souls, emptied of self, shall ascend and move through the final stages--out through the "door". But God shall supply the strength. Simplify your lives. Be not anxious about what you will wear or eat. Open instead to receive inner guidance.

Now we are calling into your remembrance all the things that your souls have Known before. Now is the time to claim them. Be of one spirit, be of one mind,be of one brotherhood of love. Your Holy Scripture will now come to be a living reality, and in each of you the Spirit shall bear witness to the truth. It is most important to read these and take them unto yourselves. They were not so much for the time in which they were written as preserved for you for this age so that they would speak living words into the cells of your being at the time of the change-over. The time of the Passover brought thanksgiving for those who were preserved as God moved against those not of His plan and spared those moving with it. So it is now. Those moving against His plan shall be bypassed and left to go down with the false creation that they encouraged. But another age shall follow this one and their time shall yet come.

Just as Christ talked very naturally and easily with his disciples in the upper room before he went to the place prepared for him, so are you ready for upper room instruction. And you have angels in your midst and disciples throughout the country--those that follow in varying degrees. The ones that move into the upper room and focus upon their Lord, watching expectantly for his return, are being gathered together for a purpose. You are being prepared so that you know and not be surprised, prepared to enter into the Kingdom with him. Learn to walk in faith and this shall produce stability within you, bring you the courage to control yourselves and all around you--the Father in you taking control because you allow it. When you, in consciousness, claim that oneness with him and you speak the command in his name, then control and order come into your lives--control of your habits and your envirorment, according to the quiet reaching toward the higher intelligence within you. You shall then follow your Shepherd by following through in an orderly life, made ready for him.

Bless you, beloveds. You are quiet and attentive, and deep in your hearts you are receptive. The indwelling Spirit is filling you. We thank you for your cooperation with us, for our love is with you and we would see you come into that joy and fullness. Our blessings go with you. Selah.
