## DISCOURSE No. 10

## <u>The Lifting Veil</u>

<u>Scripture Reading:</u> II Corinthians: 20-25 (J. B. Phillips)

Blessings be unto you, beloveds. We are keeping in close attunement with you, for the sessions being so often and close together, and the meditation and prayer from this point keeps an open door so that we may readily attune with you. You have come apart from the world, as such, leaving that of the material behind you. You come into the garden of refreshment, of spiritual lifting, and as you come together in collective consciousness, offering it unto God, this vortex intensifies light energies and power. This makes for perfect attunement and blessings of Heaven shall be yours.

We have spoken to you on a variety of subjects, and yet first and foremost we are reminding you that you are the disciples of your Lord this day, and you are following through with that which began long ago. You are the harvest of that crop planted 2000 years ago--that which your Lord and Master began to sow in the fertile field. Through the years, through many incarnations, he has raised you up through his love and power. Now you are the graduating class of this harvest age. There are many of the remnant on Earth--the remnant spoken of in your Holy Writings--those who would endure unto the end of this great age. You are to be part of that millennial reign coming to pass in the Age of Light, this next turn of the century.

As the tribulations take place through the end of this century, you have few years left to make ready and be acceptable, in every way, to your Lord. You have been chosen to follow through with that which you yourselves elected to do. You have been accepted for that assignment, and yet the growing is becoming more painful because you are coming closer to your goal. You have many hardships and trials to face in this last incarnation of the age so that you can step off the wheel of birth and rebirth and go into the state prepared for you that will be a true resurrection. Above all things pray that the kingdom come quickly, that all needs be met at one time, and the life of abundance shall be given you--all things be added unto you.

We have brought in the Bible passages to help clarify your understanding of the approaching ships--the celestial ships coming in your skies. They have been waiting until it dawned on your consciousness that they are part of the divine intervention. They wait until the day when, at the Lord's command, all shall descend at once, for the time when you shall be lifted up into the clouds to meet Him and be preserved in the celestial ships--the Ark of your day--until the Earth stabilizes from the various cataclysms. We have tried to allay your fears, to give you strength and courage to meet all that is coming and to keep your focus on that higher Kingdom. This should bring joy to your hearts! When you see that into which you are moving you will not so greatly regret that which you are leaving, or that which shall crumble before your eyes. For we have told you many times that while God created all, it was Lucifer who, in self-will, brought forth the chaos. You came under that influence and helped to produce more. This is the false system, the false kingdom that will have to fall. Out of the ashes shall come forth the new, and order shall be restored upon Earth.

The very Earth shall shake with the cataclysms which are to come, but as always, God's mercy provides for those who serve His purposes and His will. This is for the ultimate good of those working in the Father's plan, those who have humbled themselves and have chosen to be a part of His plan, chosen to allow His will to direct rather than the human will. And so you are among those who are called the remnant, those who, with faith and prayer, have endured unto this day. Thus you are being made ready to enter into the Kingdom of Spirit, the kingdom of Heaven on Earth. The veil is becoming increasingly thinner between dimensions. One day you shall have no difficulty seeing into the fourth dimension, because the rate of vibration in the atoms of the cells of your being are being speeded up, and as you move into the greater frequency of Light, your eyes will be opened more and more.

You may be surprised with the more recent changes, and our references to the early churches in your Holy Writings, but we know of no other way to convince you of the reality of your identity. As the soul remembrance is brought to the surface there is a break-through into the outer consciousness. You finally grasp the idea; the concept takes on new meaning for you. You are fulfilling the Father's plan--the begotten sent forth in His image to be raised up unto Christ-hood--following the one who first became Christ, head of the entire body of His true church. Thus in carrying you back it is almost like regression.

First we carried you, if you will remember, back, back to Adam in the garden, to the point of divergence. Spiritual man was functioning in this planet under God's laws, taking dominion, raising up the consciousness of the earth races and the animal kingdoms. But when the divergence came he used his free will and chose to enter the dream state where the self would draw power unto itself for self gratification--moving away from the God-center.

Then your Lord returned unto Earth. He was bringing the focus back to the true Center, turning attention to the Kingdom of God within. Those that had eyes to see and ears to hear followed Him in this concept, even though they did not have full understanding. They believed that the power of God was available in each, and began to live as though they believed it. That is what we have been urging you to do more and more. You are, to a great extent, but there must be more effectiveness, and there will be as we help you to identify yourselves with Adam and with the children of Israel, the lost tribes of Israel dispersed over the earth, who lost their identity, forgetting that they were the sons of God.

All this has happened to you, and with your awakening you come back into a great realization of much of this. You know now; you have been called to remember that you are, literally, sons of God strayed from the Father. You were of the house of Israel--that great house of those sent forth, begotten in God's image, to cover the earth. Though they forgot their identity and were separated from one another, still at the end of the age, all things were to be brought to remembrance; they would remember their true identities.

So you are to identify with that. You know to a great degree who you are, the home you left--the cosmic realms to which you will return after you have finished your commission. During the thousand-year millennium, as you work in the restoration of this kingdom--new heavens and new Earth--then shall come your second resurrection. But, while this is taking place, we must constantly remind you and remind you until you actually identify with the part and remember it.

Naturally, the mortal mind would say, "Why so much repetition?" It would say that this sounds like preaching. Beloveds, we are only following our Lord and your Lord, and what He gave was the reality of Life Eternal for the individual soul. No matter what you call it, it is time now for you to return to it, to choose your kingdom consciously, as your souls have chosen to go into the Kingdom of God. As we call you to identify more and more with that which you were then, that which you are now, you are being prepared, are working toward the attainment of the perfect being which God shall raise up in the Christ nature of you, the divine nature that takes dominion. Then shall you reach the point where you will be changed in the twinkling of an eye, and lifted to meet Him in the clouds while the greater tribulation is completed. Lucifer shall be bound for the thousand years, and you shall come back and serve on Earth in His Kingdom.

There is so much that you must realize before these few years are past, for you will not take part in the great day of judgment that is for Lucifer and those that have chosen his way. But you will have a certain amount of tribulation, and you are to be fortified now, that you be able to help those who are weak, who have not yet gained enough strength in their souls, yet who desire to be a part of their Lord's Kingdom. Always are there those who go before as the pioneers or explorers. You are of that group which has offered to serve.

Many are confused and think that this means they must immediately go out into some church or some group and become an officer. While there has been a place for this in the past there is not much time left. This is no longer so important, for many of man's systems and organizations which he himself has called "church" will fold, and God's true Church will be completed, will be raised up to serve Him in the Divine Plan. Thus, while we do not deny that there is certainly some work being done on Earth that shall further God's purposes, this will be secondary to the greater work He shall give you to do. You are now being conditioned, even as your astronauts are conditioned before they orbit the Earth. We work with many things--your endocrine glands--the chemical balance and the equalizing of the power and energies within your being. We help fortify your souls, ever strengthening and feeding.

Did not Jesus say unto Peter, "Do you love Me? Then feed my sheep!" The feeding has been going on, but now there is a need for even greater feeding, for in order to meet what is coming you must become strong in the power of the Lord. Those who have not yet decided which side of the fence they shall be on are not going to be strong enough to lead the way. The Shepherd is the great way-shower, and he shall return to take the government of the Earth upon his shoulders. He shall lead the way, bringing you back to Earth when the stabilizing has taken place after the comet side-swipes the planet, shifting the poles and burning one-third of the Earth's surface. After this the Lord shall return with his host and with those he has gathered into the clouds with him, and set up the new Kingdom on Earth. If this Kingdom is to be by the year 2000, do you not see how much has to take place in these last thirty-three years? And this with the greater part of it at the end of the century.

The part you are concerned with now is the first half of this remainder. Many will go before then, but whichever way your Lord calls, you must be prepared for that to which He calls you. There will not be time then for more preparation; it is now that the preparation is taking place in many ways. We have given you much of this before, and we continue to work with you; guardians walk by your sides ministering unto you as He would have them do. They are unknown to your consciousness many times for you would not understand and the curiosity of your minds would cause interference. But within, your hearts are being transformed. You are to be alert to certain attitudes; be in prayer and meditation more and more. Make more time for this.

Come ye apart from the world until finally you can obey your Lord, turn loose and be no longer earth-bound. Many would obey their Lord today if he met their conditions. It is for you to meet his conditions: give up self and bring all your affairs into order as quickly as you can. Have no panic, no fear, but in orderly fashion trust your Lord.
