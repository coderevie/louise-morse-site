MORSE FELLOWSHIP

The Morse Fellowship, a nonprofit cor-
poration, is dedicated to bringing to the
attention of all who sincerely seek spir-
itual enlightment, the deep and anointed
teachings received through the nondenom-
inational"Portals of Light": ministry of
Reverend Louise Morse Spence.

It was founded by Louise Morse in
1959, in Silver Spring, Maryland. She
and her husband, Elwood Morse, spent many
years seeking God's will, prayerfully
studying the truths and principles given
in the Holy Bible, and following the
teachings of our Lord, Jesus Christ. Wis-
dom was also conveyed to Louise by the
Holy Spirit and some of the Angelic Host,
who made themselves known to her.

In 1961 Louise moved the Fellowship
to Alamogordo, New Mexico, three years
after the unexpected passing of Elwood
from the physical plane. The work expan-
ded and flourished as Louise travelled
back and forth across the country, min-
istering to groups and individuals who
were thirsty for these truths, and spir-
itual understanding. Dedicated volun-
teer helpers maintained the Fellowship
business at the Home Center, and publish-
ed the lessons while she was absent.

to Canada, and retreats were held in
that country each summer for the next
four years. Louise met Jim Spence in
Canada, and it became apparent that

MORSE FELLOWSHIP

This is the publishing arm of the
"Portals of Light" Ministry. It is in-
corporated as a non-profit organization
under Federal and State laws, and as such
can accept gifts, contributions, trusts
and bequests, and issue tax-deductible
receipts. Indeed, it is such gifts and
contributions that pay for all the sup-

plies and equipment needed to publish
the lessons.

A list of the Fellowship publications
will be sent upon request. There are
Many volumes in print, as well as in-
dividual lessons. Tapes of the several
hundred sessions are also available, or
they may be borrowed from the Fellowship.
All lessons and tapes are given, to those
requesting, on a voluntary gift or love
offering basis. This is a work of the
Lord, and the Holy Spirit moves and prompts
those who are open to support it. Our
costs are approximately 50¢ per lesson,
$5.00 per volume, and $3.00 per tape.

May you respond to the Holy Spirit
as you read the lessons, for it is by

the Spirit that all things are revealed
unto us.
