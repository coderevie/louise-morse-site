We know that some of the Scripture read tonight has been read before, but we would have you concentrate on this process of change that is taking place to help you to realize that you are still clay being molded and reshaped. We remind you that the very life forces moving through you are bringing about this great change which is giving you, at times, strange feelings and sensations. As we have told you before, you are going through a metamorphosis and you might say you are gradually shedding an old skin, old clothes for new.

Not knowing the ways and means of how it comes about, you are apt to become confused. But when you keep true to the course you have chosen and focus on the Lord you serve, desiring to be filled with the love of God, all these things bring about a natural process through which you move as the change gradually takes place.

You are not to be concerned with the later final change at the end, the change "in the twinkling of an eye." God will see to this. We are helping you to understand what is taking place within you now, as you gradually work through the present change-over.

The cells of your bodies are changing, as well as some of your attitudes, bringing equilibrium or balance within. You are feeling new and strange things because spiritually you are coming alive. Finally you will slide out of the old cocoon and emerge, a new and shining being, but you are conscious while this is taking place.
