Blessings be unto you, beloveds. We have lifted you into attunement so that you all be as one in higher consciousness. We shall strengthen and replenish the channel as we proceed. There has been great concentration of power, energies moving through which put a strain upon the physical body, because it is of a greater concentration than previously. The body will become better conditioned to stepping into further dimensions, for the soul is being led into far greater light than at earlier times. Thus it is God's mercy that develops a soul to go out to meet the frequencies of Light, rather than have those from other realms go into lower vibrations, There is a meeting place, a meeting point, but at the same time the soul must reach out in development and expansion of its own-- that of the Christ nature within. This makes the contrast even greater, more painful in the third dimension when the soul returns, but it is part of the developing and unfolding of a. soul being brought into the higher nature and finally sustained in its eternal state.

Each of you will experience more as you develop further. Many times this seems to bring difficulties on the Earth plane. This is part of the price one pays, part of the sacrifice that love and desire will bring as the soul moves through in fulfillment. But love lifts and one finally becomes stable and is sustained in the higher nature--that body which shall be invulnerable when all is accomplished. Just as the physical body stands up under many tests and strains, so must the soul-body. That which was read previously is exactly the realization that you must come into: that the foundation was laid by the Father sending you forth, begetting of His nature. This, the "begotten son", is the Christ nature of God. So realize the foundation is there, the Divine Mind is in you. Then build upon that foundation.

If your desire is truly the fulfillment of God's plan for that which He sent forth, then it shall be done. If your desires are for human selfexpression through the mortal mind, or the animal or emotional natures, then you shall move in that direction. You have choice, but the motives that stem from your heart, the desires from deep within--your true desires--these are the ones that shall open the gates. As long as you have that in your heart you may expect fulfillment, Love your God with body, mind and soul--every part of your being--and you will be filled with God's love.

God's love builds, and it lifts the powers and energies that are the creative force of God--yours to use under His direction. You are created from them and have access to the storehouse for a never-ending supply.
