Session in Silver Spring, Maryland

Scripture Reading: John 17

Ann, Invisible Teacher speaks through Louise:

We bless you in the name of the one whom you serve.

The great light of the Christ surrounds you and your dwelling, and shines in such a way that many of the spirit world are attracted to such centers. We have explained before to you that there are many more unseen presences than the visible ones which you have in your meeting, for those needing understanding are brought in to you to be a part of the listening and the learning for the great truths of the universe. You may not understand this but those that do not have the full understanding will feel more at home, will have fuller grasp when with Earth beings than with those of the next dimension. For many as they pass into the next plane are not fully prepared to understand the meaning of the new life. They are still clinging to things of Earth and they need tender preparation; not all by any means, but many.

You would be surprised at the thousands dwelling on the spirit side who are waiting for someone to help them, to enlighten them and to lead them. And such groups on the spirit side of life are working and bringing those into Earth circles where they might find some enlightenment to send them on the path. Even this instrument was used for many years for nothing except that work with those on the suspended planes; such workers are few. Now that your work has progressed and has gone into another initiation and you have passed through much of the foundation work, we are glad that these of the spirit are bringing many to listen. For then you are serving a double work, as you might say.

You in the group are a very important nucleus. We have been trying to show you this is many ways. Were it not for your desires, your sincerity of heart and purpose, your dedication to your work and mission, your giving up of other things that you might come together in one accord, were it not for this there would not be the center and point of light that would be the open door for the teachers to work through and to express on your plane. Neither could it be done that it would be sent out to the world, that it would become the printed material that you have so successfully brought about. None of this could be done without your assistance. Never forget this. We of other realms are grateful, for our work could not be effective were you not the willing hands that we might work through you.

The ministering of angels, the working of all those from many realms that would do the Master's bidding, must be done under the great organization, the Great White Brotherhood which extends through many realms and is working to bring about that Kingdom for which you have prayed so long. The heavenly realms will descend upon the planet Earth, but the purification must be done and that is where you now find yourselves--yes--in the midst of the great cleansing of the planet Earth. So there should be joy. Keep this uppermost in your thoughts and visions. Many are the tribulations of Earth, as your scriptures give you, but have you not been blest in spite of them? Have you not been promised that all will be well? So trust and know that the great dawning will come. Be ever mindful of the responsibility that you have for now that you are awakened souls, you must never turn back. Each one has a part in the bringing in of the Kingdom of Heaven upon the planet. Time will cease, prophecies may fail, but the planet has been promised a new order. It will be accomplished partly through those of us assisting from other realms and partly through you.

Were it not for the awakened souls, it would not be done, for it was your choosing, your awakening, your asking and seeking from the planet Earth that has opened the door to the new light. Mankind was ever given free will. That is why the answer could never be given as to when the Master would return and to when the Kingdom would be in manifestation. It was said that it would be because it was a seed planted in the heart of man but not even the angels in Heaven could know the time, for that lay in the heart of man himself. As the awakening came, as the desire and the need for that divinity came about, then did man look up, then did he start his searching, then did he call out for his Creator. That recognition, that seeking brought about the change upon Earth. Those that had been given into the keeping of the Lord Jesus were the ones who would walk the Earth until the seed became fruit. They, which means you, were given to him to nourish that they might grow in the likeness of the Christ. The blueprint was within.

So in your growth you are reaching the place, calling out for your Master, knowing that you have a home to which you may return, that all will be prepared for you, yet also knowing that you are asking for the heavens around the Earth to descend, that the third dimension be spiritualized, be made whole, that the Earth no longer be in travail, but that birth be given to a new world, to new creatures, to new life, that it move in that orbit which brings it into the great dawning light which is greater than you can imagine. You did not know that which you were seeking but you had the seed within you, the desire that would grow and become restless and reach out, struggle and call forth. Yes, you were the same generation that walked the Earth with the Master and that generation of people has not passed away and will not until the harvest be reaped.

You may wonder why we repeat so much and emphasize so much the harvest. For thousands and thousands of years, for eons, life has been manifesting upon Earth, all has been moving in the direction of the change which must be at each cycle. Preparation was made through many years. Now is the time for which you are made ready. So now it is your turn to be about your Father's business. For you are placed in the heavens around the Earth that you be a part of the plan of lifting the Earth into the higher vibrations. As you failed in the beginning and descended into those vibrations of physical Earth, you were delayed in your mission. You became one with those that were Earth beings. You were the spiritual creation, created separate, a little lower than the angels, yet three cubits above the Earth, a special creation for a special mission.

No longer do you have to fail. Now you are bringing about the fruits but you can be even more effective and you will be given more opportunity, not only in this transition of changing into the fourth dimension and of helping establish the Kingdom of Heaven upon Earth, but then as you are translated and moved into the next world (as you would speak of it) the next dimension, the paradise state, then will you have a millennium of work in a higher realm. You need not be distressed at this point for when that moment comes you will know what to do and you will be working in your greater self and not in the human failings. And now you are in the midst of the quickening and changing which will get you to a point where you can be immediately taken into the next dimension. Then is the prophecy fulfilled, that you be changed in the twinkling of an eye. When you reach that stage then this may be done. And part of our work is to help you to reach that place, that it be not a sudden change and shock for you but that you be easily transported and changed into that greater self that you are.

Would it not be foolish for the caterpillar to question the change from the cocoon, or to question the freedom of life as the beautiful butterfly that is released into the light, into the glorious world, the different vision that you can imagine at the first flight of the butterfly? Would it not have a new vision of light than that when it was crawling upon the ground or upon the leaf of the bush, or yet while it was embryonic in the cocoon? Well, beloveds, do you not see that you are resting in the cocoon? Can you not have faith that you will soon be in a bright and glorious and free existence in a new realm? Does not that bring joy to your heart? Would you retard this with fear or distressing thought or anxiety?

You should be giving thanks, you should be singing praises with joy that before too long there would be your release and you become the beautiful butterfly in a new light, in a new life, with no limitations. Would that we could help you to understand and to see this, that all anxiety would leave you, that you be the willing hands while you are upon the Earth in the third dimension and that you joyfully bide your time until you take flight. All your labors would seem to you in a different light did you have full comprehension of this. Your problems would seem as nothing for the old world is truly passing away as your words of wisdom have told you. Cling not to things of the past. Look to that light into which you are going. Know that many glories await you.

And now the only important thing is that you be a part of understanding that you might pass this love and trust and peace to your neighbor. Love your neighbor as yourself, yes, but share with your neighbor that peace and joy that is reality, not words, but a faith that will hold until the time of the transition that will carry you through the dark days, knowing that you have the light within you. Do not fear and tremble when the clouds are dark around you. You have the secret, you have the key. Your consciousness can penetrate the darkness for the light of God is within you. You need not seek the skies. Let that light shine through you that the world may see the divinity in and through and around you. You are the representative of the Most High God. Take that responsibility not in vain. You are the bearer of that portion of light that will lift the world.

These are not idle words, beloveds. They are the truth of the universe. They are directing you to the centers of the light that will fill the world. The laws of the heavens are operating throughout the physical world. You have the consciousness, you have the contact that you might call radio wires or radio wireless. You can know the truth, for as you open your soul to the light, not an intangible light, but a light of reality, then you are becoming another channel, another instrument. And when enough individual lights are shining, will not the dark planet become light? How else did you think it would happen? True it is that vibrational spheres will have changed, that the Earth will enter another orbit, but the true lighting of man will come from within each individual, of those that are appointed to be the followers of the Christ light.

Did the Master not say that he was the light of the world? Did he not say that you would do even greater things than he and that you were one with him as he was one with the Father? When you have true realization of this you forget your human self, you forget that ego that you have been living under and you recognize the image of God within you and then do you see that the light of God will come through each one that He has made upon Earth. The leavening is taking place. Much has been done on Earth. The way has been cleared, things will move rapidly, changes are coming about every day. But keep in mind; walk in humility, walk in faith, understanding that you know what is going on, that you know all is being moved for good and that you are a part of it. Therefore, you can say to your neighbor, "Arise, come forth and live for all is not lost but has been found. Follow the light and it will lead you home."

Those looking to you will follow your light. There are many that may never see the light of God unless they see it in you. Does this not give you a new incentive? Does it not cause you to see the importance of each soul upon Earth? You are not your own, you are the master plan walking the Earth in human flesh. Relax, let go, be willing and see the divine plan work out through your being and that of each one who has awakened to the point that they say, "Here am I, take me and let me be the light for the one coming after."

It is so vital that the world understand what is happening to it. If this were something that would be centuries from now there would not be so much emphasis. There was the emphasis given the few almost two thousand years ago. The seeds were planted, the word was spoken, the cause was set in motion. But you are bringing it about, you are part of the fruition and in between there has not been so much given. But was it not said that in the last days of the age that the spirit of truth would be poured out upon all flesh? And yet many turn away and recognize it not.

You are not left alone. Thousands and thousands, legions of angels surround your planet, all working in course of action, waiting for the moment that the final deliverance will be, sending forth hope and new truth, patiently looking on, inspiring those who would be inspired, those who are willing, those who seek, bringing forth inventions to men of science who wait in the stillness of the morning for the secrets that come to them. You have not been alone. Yet but a little while and you will know more of the ethereal ships surrounding your planet and waiting for the time at hand when the divine intervention only comes trhough your request and your prayers and desires.

Do you not see that this is a very important point in your prayer life? For many are the misunderstandings in the world, many would say it does no good to pray for the laws of God are unchangeable. But the great laws take into account all things that will be, all changes, all situations and beforehand are the answers there. Now when you stop to think that your prayers are thought taking wings, going into the ether, reaching out a sincere desire of the soul, searching, seeking, asking, and opening the door, do you not see then that those legions of angels work ever to obey the Lord sending them. those in high command under the plan and great love of the Almighty? Then each one is ministered to by the angels, by the guardians appointed him and as you send out great desires for the soul of you contact is made. You are reaching out to those who have been commanded to serve you and many are the ways that they can guard you, can guide you, can teach you and lift you up and sustain you.

So then will you come with questions of desires, with childlike prayers of faith. It does not matter if you use wrong words, if you do not have the same pattern, the same understanding. All in the heavens know the meaning of the desire of the soul and it matters not whether you are in certain ones of your churches or synagogues or temples or out by the lake sitting quietly in meditation. Anywhere that you are, the desire going from the soul made in the image of the Father is going out, out into the ether to be received, and in the receiving of this, instantly there are angels--guardians--and at times even some of your loved ones who have lived on Earth with you, depending on the occasion and the need and the one to help. All these can take place and can change the circumstances around you or can change your thoughts or your actions to a certain degree. Keeping in mind that you have free will so that you are master of your fate, you can delay action or you can receive according to your free will of the soul.

These are the changes that can take place--not that the great laws of the universe are changed--for these are not but your Divine Father had made it possible that many are those serving Him and serving you in His name, even as we come from other realms apart from Earth because we have chosen to serve in the capacity where we may be most helpful. Thus are we sent and appointed to certain ways and places and groups of Earth. And so it is with those in the world of spirit. The spiritual beings will come, guardian angels will come at your bidding. Many are at your service in His name even as you. So you may see the possibilities of many ways that you are helped. And is it not all because the Father knew that you would have need and so ordained this way before you were placed in paradise, that all would be possible whatever your needs might be? So no great laws are changed. They are made that always mercy will be available, always will you have these to assist you when you ask and pray and seek.

Does this not make prayer a different picture to you now when you ask, you seek that a need be met and it matters not your mistakes for it is the desire that sends it forth. If you pray amiss you will be guided and shown. When you ask, you will be shown your greatest need. It is not always that which you think it might be so in opening the door, in making yourself a completely clear channel, then will the desire of the soul be sent forth and the needs of the flesh and the soul will be visible to those assisting you and the needs will be met. You may lie in peaceful state without uttering a word and yet the soul will be expressing a prayer, a desire, a need and instantly that this is sent forth it is received by those who have given themselves to you in service that they might hold you up, that you stumble not. But you close the door, you block the assistance by your anxiety and your fears and your doubts and your discords.

If all the religious and spiritual groups of the world today would stop their disagreeing, their discordant notes, their quarreling, their dissension, their jealousies, fi they would stop all and just sit back and say, "Lord, here am I--let your will be done through me." If they would do this and truly trust, leaving out the fear and anxiety and doubts, then would all be lifted up. For the Lord cares not by what name or organization you go, by what creeds or doctrines you profess, it is only that you clear the way, that the light shine through and that the power of the Creative Spirit can operate through you. That is the important thing. All else will be added. Have you not been told, "Seek first the Kingdom of Heaven" and that, you are told, is within you? Seek that open door, seek that light that is buried within you. Do not put it out but let it shine that you glorify your Maker. May your hearts have understanding, may you ponder these, go back and remember. The spirit of you is important to the completion of the mission on Earth.

Bless you, beloveds. Thank you, thank you for your trust.

Question: Could you give us a description of what is conceived in higher realms which one contemplates or one meditates?

Answer: With concentration you focus intently upon a point of thought or light or picture or that image that you would hold to and sustain a path. With concentration there is a straining as a rule and you cannot hold concentration too long. With contemplation you are thinking with your soul. You are not holding to one point and one thought and sustaining it. You are trying to evalute the spiritual. You are contemplating an ideal, an inage, or you are placing yourself in that state of passivity. Yes, in your contemplating God, for instance, you are opening your soul to understanding. You are looking and wondering and yet you are not in that state of nirvana.

In meditation you actually are listening and becoming receptive; you are doing something or sending out, either looking or thinking in the other two; and in praying you are speaking to your Lord, you are seeking and asking and talking with him, laying bare your soul. In meditation you should be listening for your answer. In meditating you should be still in the presence of your Lord, stilling your thoughts and listening for the answer that would come through.

Question: Does that mean that you could work both at the same time, contemplate and then meditate, listening?

Answer: Yes, then you are truly opening the doors and there is a sending out at a given time whether it be words or the thoughts of the soul or questions. There is a communication you send out, you are seeking, you are asking, or praying, or thinking, or looking at an object--whatever it might be. In meditation you are quiet, you still your conscious mind, you relax and you become almost numb. You let go completely and expectantly listen. First remind yourself and be aware that you will receive an answer and then listen for the speaking of your heart. For you will be shown whether it be a picture, or vision or dream, revelation, or whether it be words given you. If you ask earnestly and you sit quietly, expecting, then you listen and you will receive. But remember, this must be correct meditation; it must not be with thoughts in your mind. It must be practiced with discipline, day after day at the same time, preferably in the same place but at least something that will become automatic, that will be auto-suggestion for you.

You go even as the example of the instrument now at the given time when there is to be a session, or a reading, or a group meeting.
