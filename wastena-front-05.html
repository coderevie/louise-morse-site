THE WASTENA RETREAT

Forward

"Wastena", an Indian name which means "sparkling waters", is the name of a large, comfortable summer home which is located on a small lake on an island off the northern shore of Lake Ontario in Canada. It is a beautiful setting, one which lends itself to quiet introspection or meditation. The owners, dedicated people who are readers of Morse materials, and who have been hosts to the channel, Louise Morse (Spence), when she traveled to Canada on several occasions, were led to offer Wastena to the Fellowship for approximately four weeks in the summer of 1967, to be used for a spiritual retreat.

A better place could not be imagined. It is set among towering pines, surrourided by flowers and is situated at the edge of the "sparkling waters" of the little lake. It is a place of peace and tranquility. The Cosmic Teachers said, in confirming it as a place of retreat, that it is a2 area of great intensity of Spirit, highly charged for upliftment of soul.

Plans were made and subsequently carried out, resulting in the Wastena Retreat of liorse Fellowship. People came and went as they were led, quietly, informally but in an orderly manner which lent itself to the occasion. Group sessions were held twice a day during which the Cosmic Teachers spoke through the channel. Thirty-three discourses were given in all, one for each year of the Master's life on Earth.

In the three-week period, thirty-five people attended the retreat, some staying a few days, some attending each weekend, and a few able to be present the entire time. The greatest number in any one day was nineteen. Those who were privileged to attend will readily attest to the power of Spirit present.

The sessions carried a sense of urgency: that man must turn more toward his God and take immediate steps to reduce his involvement in worldly matters.

May all who read or listen to these discourses be inspired to grasp, in part at least, their deep significance and be blest as were those who attended.
