Scripture Reading: 6th Chapter of John

Our greetings to you, beloveds. We have remained in attunement with you in this place where the vibrations are high. When your focus is upon the higher attainment it is easy to come into your midst, At this time, we will try to help clarify that which was read from your Holy Writings and we would speak with you about the great contrast between the expanded consciousness and the inflated ego.

You are growing in the Christ nature but you have not come forth from the human nature, for you do not yet understand the difference, nor do you understand that which brings growth in each. For so long you have been fattening the human ego, fostering human weakness, allowing the human will to become stronger and stronger. You are constantly feeding one or the other. Through a lack of understanding of these words from your Bible you have overlooked completely the way in which the feeding takes place. It has been easy for man to inflate his ego; the human will has grown stronger because he fostered everything in that nature. He fed upon that which he looked at, focused upon, entered into.

Mind is truly the builder. Your mortal mind, through imagination, can carry you into all kinds of situations brought about by your ideas and desires of the lower nature. Divine mind can help you focus on your true goal, the heart's desire. The soul chooses one way or the other and, starting toward its goal, can become distracted as you shift your focus and start moving into something else.

The body of Jesus was that which he laid down to save all. He put down the human ego, that he rise up in the Christ nature to reveal and demonstrate the great law unto others. He did this so that they would understand that he gave them the blueprint for that within themselves. Through this demonstration he offered the way to all. Those who will identify with that consciousness, in discernment recognizing that principle in operation, have the key to the working of the power of God within them as well as to becoming Christed. This brings fulfillment. “Feeding,” then, is moving into the life energies which would draw one into that fulfillment. }

God is Spirit. You actually feed upon the air you breathe in the third dimensiones You are taking it in, feeding on it, moving in it. When you move into the higher dimension, you will not feed on matter or oxygen--anything of the third dimension, You will instead move into that which is God-law in action, feeding on the increased light of the higher dimension. When the Master came down from high planes of the cosmic realm, he brought the energies of Life to restore souls that were fast weakening and dying. They were strengthened by identifying with his loving sacrifice, in thanksgiving opening their souls and hearts. In this way you can identify with the true life of the Christ. He overcame the grave but he transcended matter. He took on the glorified body, in that body feeding on Light--the atoms of creative energy which give the supply of substence to the soul.
