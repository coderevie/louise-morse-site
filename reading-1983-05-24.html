Establish A Communication Within

MORSE READING

May 24, 1983

ESTABLISH A COMMUNICATION WITHIN

Yes, we have been with you all the way. But it does take more time than you realize to still all the cells of your bodies, your minds and the thoughts of the day. And at times there are those things to be discussed or settled ahead of time. For remember always, communication is truly one of the most important things to be considered. If you realize communication with your creator is first of all, you commune with Him, you communicate, you bring out and tell Him of your needs, not that He needs your telling, but you need your telling. For as you release, send them out, send them forth, it is therapy for you. And the wisdom of God knows this. And as He communicates with you, in accordance to your yielding and sending out your needs, getting rid of pride, getting rid of the many things that would block and interfere with straightforward communication and communion. As you go into the higher planes you think of it as communion. But it is communication between God and man, and a listening and learning to be quiet, turn within into the center of your being, go into your closet to pray to that within you, the Holy Temple and the Holy of Holies in the center of your being and learning to communicate in many ways with your God. And even that which is known as speaking in other tongues, or unknown tongues is a part of that because when the spirit prays unto God, that is in the infinite wisdom far beyond your mortal minds when your mentality does not always know what is needed or the best way in which to pray. You do the best you can with it in the finite mind. But in the infinite there is the freedom for the spirit prays unto God with no interferences, no static, no pride, no emotions of the mortal. So there is a great advantage. It is more difficult than one realizes to free the body of the tensions of the day. Too much speed, too much momentum is taking place as all this acceleration of energies continues and will even more so and be beyond that which your body and mind could keep pace. There are so many hidden and unseen things and unrecognized things pertaining to the body and mind and the soul preparation for entering into the courts of the Lord. As you begin to let it well up within you, the thanksgiving, the gratitude, the blessings sent forth, with all of these things your soul comes into alignment with the spirit. And, of course, the body and mind have to learn to control themselves and to be quiet, be stilled in order that the soul can choose and come into alignment with the spirit and the spirit one with Jesus, Jesus one with the Father. Therefore, through Him you can attain to that oneness with the Father. And it is certainly more than just a quick change of attitude. It calls for much more than that. So many things take place from the unseen side (unseen to you) that is far more going into operation than you realize when you come together for a meeting. So learn to be patient even more than you have been. Learn to begin to control your body in the spirit of you taking authority in the spirit as we have shown you before.

There is a simplicity toward following the laws of God and coming into a closeness with Him. But yet it is through obedience to many things and yet you enter into that smooth way of coming into oneness. Never is it what the human or the mortal mind thinks it is. There is so much more involved as you yield yourself to your God, as you humble yourself before Him. It is the soul coming forth. And as it grows in spirit and feeds on the manna from heaven, then gradually it begins to show in the outer manifestation in the physical body and mind and actions of the being. That is why many times it takes longer than you realize to get each and every one of you in the same stillness and alignment. You see, there could be half of you meeting the conditions, and for one reason or another possibly the other half or some several might not be within their being. They could be aiming for it and trying to and wanting to, but that is where the help from heaven comes in. As you sit patiently waiting heaven comes in to intervene. There are billions of cells in your body and you have them come to instant control in several galaxies within that body form. Many are the conditions to be met. And when celestial ones come into your plane there are changes that have to take place with them to enter into your atmospheric plane and to help you to meet the conditions necessary for the higher attunement. Certainly you can come in and sit down and hear someone talk, that is not the whole story. These are getting to the finer points within in the higher purpose. There are many thousands of meetings going on all over the world, but a meeting is a meeting. An attunement with God is one thing. Developing and lifting the consciousness of your being requires various conditions within. It is not something you can write out from one to ten and say, these are the things you could do. It is not that way. It is an infilling of the spirit that you have emptied the temple of self and with practice more and more and cultivating, it becomes more readily achieved. Angels stand by loving you, praying for you. Yes, angels pray unto God.

And there is a great coordination of heavenly work of helping those in this third dimension of yours. It is not all that easy to step from heavenly realms into your world. Such a great contrast you could not imagine. That is one reason it is difficult for you to imagine the heavenly spheres and believe them to be true for they are foreign to you. You have become so accustomed to the world with its snares and pitfalls, pollution and corruption. And all of the heavens are obediently working with the Lord to bring about your transformation, to help you to reach a step by step to a point where you can become extricated from this as you might call it, "nightmare" of the world. Yes, you have been living out a dream. In many cases, it could be called a "nightmare" and in other things they could be good, but yet not heavenly. You will be greatly surprised and pleased when you actually enter into God's heavens prepared for you in His Kingdom. They're not words, but in reality into which you are moving. And certainly, you may say, "Praise the Lord for this," and "thank God for this," as do we all, for no one would want to remain midst the fallen race on a corrupt planet. Surely you may use that expression, "Well, you get used to it after a while," but you do not need to become used to it and have no excuse for not making ready for the change. Desiring within you to seek the Kingdom of God above all else is the most important thing that could ever be on the earth next to your faith race. For then all needs will be met and God will bestow blessings far beyond anything you could imagine upon the earth. But the expectancy is needed. It is part of His plan that you realize, choose it, work toward it, expect it. Look for Him to come for you and obey the laws along the way that would make it more acceptable to you and more accessible, getting rid of much debris mentally and physically, materially. Be willing to humble yourself before God, literally, without pride or arrogance or the things of the human nature; let these go. And surely believe it that God's Kingdom has far greater than anything the earth offers you. Become not only hopeful, but expectant within you eagerly, anxiously awaiting Him and His Kingdom and be ready to do what He calls for while yet on this planet and the things that you are called to leave behind you. Do not think that you will miss anything on this earth, for in truth it will grow strangely dim as you come closer to realizing the joys and the beauties and the light of heaven and come face to face with your Lord. You will, then, be glad to put things behind you. They're no longer necessary. And for the most part, you have been living with substitutes. When you enter into the reality of God's Kingdom that is so great, so beautiful, so powerful, so complete that you will rejoice indeed. And you will have no desire to look back to the things of the world.

And in readiness for this you begin to make many preparations for certainly the time is short. The many cycles that come to pass and the many, many of these nineteen year cycles for ages have been taking place, have moved on closer and closer and closer to the return of Jesus, the Lord of the earth and its heavens. So do not feel sad when you see the results of the cause and effect of a false kingdom. It has to crumble and fall. God has allowed it to run its course. But anything that is poisoned, polluted and only bringing destruction, can you not then rejoice when it is being cleared out, cleansed, fallen, destroyed. Just as you see many old, old buildings condemned for they're infested with rats, or termites or insects and a fire trap ready to fall, ready to bring destruction on any one that would be within them and they're condemned and then torn down, the land cleared for new building. If you have memories about earlier times in connection with that building you might feel a certain sadness, but still you would let it go, be glad that now something new would rise up in its place. This is the way you must feel about the world and all of the calamities upon it. It will be changed, cleansed, purified by Jesus the Anointed One who is going to establish His Kingdom upon a cleansed earth. And those that have been called up into the clouds to meet Him having a preparation during the seven years, the great tribulation, or the final consequences upon the earth. And so having been told about that which is to come they will return with Him. This will be you, with many others to come back as He sets foot upon the earth, a cleansed and changed earth fit for the manifestation of His Kingdom under Almighty God's plan. What better hope could there be, far better than any new administration coming in as president of your country, or king of other lands, for there is only a slight hope and then another disappointment and another failure and continuing on to the way the world has been. The greed of man, the self-centeredness of man, the deceit, the political angles. They can never bring peace or goodness. They have to go if you truly desire and expect the Kingdom of God to be manifested under God's plan. The old habit pattern is so strong that man still tries to seek out ways that man can repair the world and the damage, that man can fix it. Man cannot because he is human. He is that mixture full of deception, deceit, ulterior motives, love of money, love of materialism, love of all the things of the world. So until he turns loose of these and puts his love of God Almighty above everything else and willing to obey the Son that completed the mission, Jesus, one with the Father and with His plan for His creation, only then can the new kingdom be established that will have eliminated all these other things. And truth and justice and love, mercy, all the wonders, the attributes of God to take place in the new kingdom. Only then can you have peace. And those that were hungry will be fed and those that were ill will be made whole.

So as you train your thoughts and discipline your mind more in this direction, and cultivate that expectancy, only then can you begin to be a part of all that moving into the spirit overcoming, as the pattern of the old recedes, and the new comes in a great and shining light, with no darkness, no shadows. And you will indeed be transformed if not along the way, then at that instant in the twinkling of an eye when the Lord meets you face to face as you are drawn up to meet Him in the clouds. And in the meantime you can work toward overcoming with the resurrection power and that is what He gave it to you for. He was resurrected and He gave you the overcoming power the moment that you believe on Him and believe in His crucifixion and His resurrection to redeem the souls of mankind - then you have access. If you do not believe this happened, then you have no right to claim it. If you do believe it happened and believe in the Lord Jesus, the Christ, then you claim it and begin to use it and believe what He said. It is then that it becomes a reality within you and you enter into the reality of it. You move into a different plane of consciousness, a different frequency of light, and your consciousness begins to be a part of that expansion, that greater realization.

It is not only hearing about something, but it is literally leading you into the light of it. That is why Jesus could say, "I am the way to the Father, I am the truth and the light." He was experiencing it, was transformed, and went on through, His resurrection was complete and He showed you the way to what had happened. But He was willing to give up much more than most. He was willing to sacrifice. He was willing to be love. He had no home. He had no furniture. He had no schooling and He had no money, very little, but with the group. Judas was the treasurer of some that they had for then, possibly their clothing, for food. But much of the food came from the sea. Jesus went about doing the Father's business. He walked by the sea. He went into the mountain tops. He taught along the way of parables, examples from the every day world that the disciples could recognize and see from their level of living. He showed them the way day by day, but they still did not understand what He was saying for the most part. He still could not get it through to them or they did not open to receive it enough. But they still felt they must warn Him, or tell Him what to do or not do. They tried to get Him to escape and not be captured and killed. He was going by the wisdom of God. They were going by inexperience and world distortion. If He had listened to them, His closest friends, disciples, you would not have been redeemed and your soul would not be saved today. So you could certainly praise God that He listened to God instead of man. And it was hard through part of it, for He was agonizing in the Garden of Gethsemane as He prayed to let that cup pass from Him, but nevertheless He relinquished Himself unto God and said, "Thy Will be done." His disciples were not able to do that at that time. And many now as they read from the Holy Scriptures try to use their mental interpretation and put all kinds of meanings into it. They are not ready to yield, to put down their pride or all of the things of the world. Many will say, "Yes, Yes, Lord, we want to follow you," but they are not willing to make the sacrifice to be where He is, a commitment, a dedication to any part of the purpose in God's Kingdom. A commitment may be partly so. But to follow Jesus all of the way, to dedicate oneself wholly to Him and His work in the Kingdom of God these are rare, are few. For any part of His Kingdom, or any part of getting the message out to those of the world it takes deep dedication, sacrifice, yes, giving up one thing for another. But there is nothing greater than one or a team, teamwork of two, whom God brings together to work for His Kingdom. A husband and wife, working together in the same purpose, praying together and there in the midst is Jesus, the anointed One, His power, His love, His help. What greater thing on earth can there be except to follow Him in wherever the calling is. Many are called in one direction and many are called into some other part of His Kingdom. But wherever the call or call is within that one should follow with all of their heart, not being unstable or unreliable, or fickle in nature, but with integrity true to the course all to the glory of God - not accepting credit for themselves. To God goes the glory.

And angels of heaven go forth in this manner - loving their Lord, willing to obey. Celestial ones of many of the higher planes rejoice serving God. And those assigned to work with the Son, Jesus the Christ, preparing to come with Him at the Second Coming, as it is called on your plane, go all out to pave the way, to make the way possible to help those lift their consciousness that they be able to rise up to meet Him. Could you but see the thousands, thousands of celestial ones, active, busy following their Lord's direction, making ready that those redeemed from the earth plane to be broguht up into heavenly realms, be instructed and then return with Jesus, the Lord and Master, to a cleansed earth to be co-rulers in His Kingdom for a thousand years. Then many things will develop after that. You can trust the wisdom of God. It is not necessary that you know the details then. Only seek to obey that which you know now.

And as you make ready day by day in this time of the end of this world pattern, go one day at a time praying morning and evening and in between. Let God show you the way, show you how you may be used each day. Learn to simplify more and more. Rather than trying to build up treasures on earth, know that as you love Him and are following Him, you will be building up treasures in heaven, eternal ones that will not rust nor decay, but last forever. And no matter how hard you try to hold to the things of this world, they will be shaken loose from your grasp. They will not be yours for long. And that which you have in the heavenly realms will be far superior. So as you more and more practice being in the presence of the Lord, listening to the Holy Spirit in your quiet times (and more and more quiet times) then you will be surprised what you will begin to hear from the Lord, or to have revealed and shown you in one way or another. For He will lead each step of the way.

Those of you that are called to try to store some food or shelter, or clothing, bedding for a time, if you are called to do this deep within you, then by all means follow it. But not everyone is called to do that. So never should there be that time to imitate and do what someone else is doing. For each one of you is unique in God's purpose. It is different for each one. And whatever you do, it will only be temporary. But if you are being called to keep some on hand, allright for the months that might last, certainly obey the spirit within you. That is what even now is being brought about. God is desiring to have you come into training for listening to Him within, and not being so concerned with what others are teaching or preaching or receiving. What are you receiving from God? If you have that true desire, He will begin to commune with you. Establish the communication when you are alone. Talk to Him as a child would talk to his father or as one talking to his elder brother. For Jesus is your Elder Brother, one that you can truly depend on when many a time an earthly brother is not so dependable. But practice that, receiving the guidance within you. For there will come a time when that might be your only way. That time will come when you will have need of knowing the voice within, communicating, communing with the Holy Spirit within you. That is why it was promised by Jesus that His disciples wait for the Holy Spirit to come upon them. They would go forth in new power and the Holy Spirit would abide, live within that house that temple if they chose and asked for it. For never would God impose on the free will of His creation. But each one of you should and must establish within you a communication within that inner voice. You know it as conscience. But begin to recognize it as you recognize your conscience pricking you, or reminding you, and then inspiration that comes. It is fine, as you are attuned many times, and you have sightings of space ships, feel an inner communication of these. That is part of the entire system of God's plan for those that surround the earth, trying to help mankind, keep him from blowing up his planet, trying to give protection and instruction, trying to break through into the consciousness. And with practice, daily, you may think for a time that you are not receiving anything, and yet one day you will. You can every day if you stop and listen, withdraw from the world long enough to listen to God and His Holy Spirit within you. Gradually that voice will be a little louder, or a little more prominent, and you will know when you are being prompted to do one thing or another, prompted to go or to stay. You begin to feel the directions and recognize the way in which it is given.

And know that the Holy Spirit is your friend sent by Jesus, your Brother. And it is all the power of God and in the divine plan of God. Now there can be things mistaken by using the mentality in the mortal mind. Satan can put in thoughts to try to fool you or trip you up. But those are thoughts; they have a different feeling, or a different way of coming than the inspiration within you with the Holy Spirit guiding you. Learn to feel and recognize the difference. The mental is in the head. The Holy Spirit, and the oil of the lamp, is in the heart in that part of you which is the solar plexis, the opening of the door into the temple. Did not Jesus say that He stands at the door and knocks, let Him come in, that He would come in and sup with you. This is the area in which the action takes place in the temple, in the Holy of Holies within the temple, with the Holy Spirit abiding in your temple. It means living in your temple, not as a silent partner, but as one that you need to listen to, that knowledge that is there with you. And one day you will no longer need to ask how can you tell is this the Holy Spirit speaking or is it Satan tempting. Satan will tempt your mortal mind and you can answer him back by quoting scripture to him as Jesus did, or you can rebuke him and tell him to get out of your house in Jesus' name. That Name is power. So do not take it lightly. It is power and in your spirit to rise up and command something to be done in the Name of Jesus, Son of God, that is with authority. And that which is not of God must go. In that manner you protect the door of your temple; you keep the money changers out. You keep the false things out, the temptations out. Just as the angel sat on a rock guarding the door at the tomb of Jesus, you guard the door. For you, too, have the many angels that could be called upon. Did not Jesus say that He could have called upon ten thousand angels to have saved Him from being crucified, but if He had He would be disobeying the plan of Almighty God. For being one with Him, He knew what that plan was, what that mission was. And He made the sacrifice, obeyed it to go on all the way through to the dark regions, through unto spiritual death to save you and all mankind from having to go for you would not have had the power to come back up from the resurrection as He did. And He could have lost; there was that chance. But He won, He came out victorious. Because He lived and was resurrected it was life for you eternally and your chance to go back into the Father's Kingdom.

So bear these things in mind and in your heart ponder them and be thankful unto God for all of them. You have not really realized who you were, how you were redeemed and saved, the greatness of it all, and the power that you acquired when Jesus was resurrected. He did it for your sake. And because He was triumphant, that gave the power to His Name and He gave you an opportunity to come forth in the spirit and claim things in that power of His Name, to go forth in great power to heal.

There are great and mighty powers within you because Jesus went to the cross and died for you all the way, the physical death, and unto the spiritual death and came through resurrected, victorious. And God the Father crowned Him King over all Kings, mighty beyond of all the begotten sons of God. He was the first to obey, go all the way in obedience, love and sacrifice and return obediently unto the Father triumphant.

So He was given all power and He sent forth the Holy Spirit to be with His disciples and with you. And all of those that believe on Him then are free from bondage. And as they obey, and choose to follow Him, then He went to prepare a place that where He was you could be also, and all of those that believed on Him. So this is indeed the mighty message to send forth to the earth - that He lives, He was triumphant, He overcame the grave, death and the grave and to send forth over the world that all men may know that He was the only one to come forth in resurrection, and to be able to redeem mankind, the lost race. And now they are found; now they are returned, restored to their inheritance, the Kingdom of God, those that believe on Him. All have that opportunity and that choice. If they choose not to believe on Him, then they are still outside of the realm of that redemption. But all have the equal opportunity to believe on Him and be raised up in a resurrection to be eternally with Him. And also that message that comes from celestial ones serving God, how they are surrounding the planet in the great celestial ships, that you call space ships, obediently showing, revealing the place where Jesus was born, the angels showing the shepherds as they were working in the field, leading them to the Savior, leading the children of Israel across the wilderness, into the promised land by a cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night, all the way through obeying God, helping mankind, calling Moses up to the mountain-top to receive the Ten Commandments written in stone, of the great light rays of the space ship, divine power, divine fire, the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah from the ships that God sent forth to destroy all of the corruption, the terrible things they had become. He warned and brought forth any that were willing to obey Him, spared them as in the time of the flood. Always will those be spared for God's purpose if they are believing Him and choosing to follow Him. This has no relation to doing good deeds, or being holy, for they certainly are not goodness itself or holy enough to justify it. But if they believe in God's purpose, and on His Son, and on the crucifixion and resurrection, then they are met by the angels and go forth with Him.

So be not weary, nor impatient. For all of the celestial ships surrounding the planet are still working toward helping mankind and helping them to meet with Jesus, serve Him, obey Him and that is the other message to get out before the world so that they will have no fear when celestial ships come. And they will be more and more aware of them. And be not afraid, be thankful unto God that they stand by, as they are doing the many things they are doing to serve Jesus the Christ. And all of the ways that you can help further this work, this mission that was brought forth to our channel, to the soul Saramyia, to go forth in the spirit. And the dedication has been complete, the faithfulness to serve God, to walk in humility, in love, patience, going through many trials and yet trusting in Jesus, the Christ, as the soul moves toward Christhood. And as each one moves toward that transformation that can take place in the twinkling of an eye, whether it be before, or at least as meeting the Lord in the air. But as one believes, makes sacrifice and loves the Lord above all else, and as there is love one for another, He is in their midst. And all of these things bring blessings in the heavenly realm. And out of many that have been helped by the many discourses to the soul through these years, always there has been the few, just as there was at the time of Jesus, the few around Him for the undergirding and walking with Him, learning, listening to that which He was teaching. Always is this needed. But those that feel the call to help further to send out to others then are the more faithful than ever to obey God and to be the next nucleus of those undergirding and supporting in prayer and energies and love, the channel and the mission to which she has given her life. It is the Lord's mission. She has been true to it, protective of keeping it pure, keeping it in the hands of honorable ones who would not misuse it. And so it is this time now. There was a prompting of the Holy Spirit to her and the prompting to the ones in this area to request that she come and that was a purpose from the higher heavens. She responded, many unselfishly assisted and did all they could to make it possible. Blessings will be upon those who serve. Blessings will be upon the mission and the lessons that were sent forth throughout these years for then and for now and for the tribulation times. And many have been prepared and sent forth as was directed to the North, South, East and West of this country and to the many other countries to which they went and many are those now that have the entire set of all discourses and would like any new ones to come. But it does take love and sacrifice and it does take financial means for any reprinting in whatever way. First of all, pray together, and separately, for the next step in this continuing of the mission through so many years. And most of those which had been published have been distributed. So whatever it takes, whether it be tapes or printed, but pray and seek first the Kingdom of God to reveal and inspire that you know the steps in which to go about it. Do not let it be the human ego running away with you. But in prayer seek inspiration, keep in communication with each other. And be willing to do your part in furthering the cause of Jesus the Christ. For it is He that first brought it forth. And God's plan arranged that the channel would be open and receptive to receive the celestial ones as they appeared to her identifying themselves as being in service to Jesus and coming with Him when He comes again which is not too far away.

So let it be that He knows and does not say when you say Lord, Lord unto Him that He does not say, I never knew you, but that He can say well done thy faithful servant, for I have known you, I have heard you and you have heard me and you have brought forth my works unto those having need in this day, patiently awaiting His return. More inspiration will come to you as you sit in prayer asking for that information and inspiration from the Holy Spirit. And doors will open and you will be able to assist in getting it forth. For our channel cannot do all parts alone on the physical plane. Even though heaven works with you and the Lord blesses you, there needs to be cooperation among the physical vessels. For God manifests through the vessel offered unto Him as He brought forth His Will through Jesus and so He does through the channel and through those that are willing to be humble enough, obedient, reliable to Him that He may use them to further His cause on earth. For God loves His people. He loves His creation and He desires that you love one another. Join hands and hearts to serve Him in obedience, expectantly watching for the return of Jesus. For your blessings will be manifold as you move into His Kingdom. Bless you beloveds and those of you who have been faithful and those of you who will be faithful for you shall feel the inspiration, the Holy Spirit calling you to be a part of the Holy mission. Bless the channel for these many years of service as each one. Bless this home and this family and may God's will be done through each of you who love Him. Selah.

So be it beloveds.

As we have also protected the channel thank you for your love and support. There are times that can be dangerous, but those that are close can be as a shield. A nervous system of an individual as it is used like an antenna with your machines, radio and television, there is a great sensitivity and an openness to help in furthering the reception, but it also opens to dangers, vulnerability. But she is allright now, thank you. And to an extent this is true with each one of you as you sit in meditation or in the spirit when it cannot always be prevented in this world. But in your heavenly kingdom, you will have only good and light and love and the wonderful ways of God. You can already begin to give thanks for that which is your inheritance even though you are not fully manifesting in it at this time, you will. That is the promise of God for all those that believe on His Son. You believe, therefore you are. He is raising up sons and daughters of God to be of His Kingdom and later on to be a part of governing His other worlds, many, many worlds. So much is before you, but there is no time, nor wasted energy, nor tiredness in that realm, so it will be only joy serving your God. And as you now are in practicing so to speak in awaiting upon the Lord, cultivating that inner recognition of His communing with you, then it will come in good stead as things begin to happen in ways which will move swiftly at that time. Then when you know His voice and you know the way He shows you and leads you, it will be to great advantage then and He will lead you home. So have no fears of that coming to pass, for guardian angels are with you constantly every day and other celestial ones assist in God's great plan for more than you realize in your mortal minds, for He loves His children greatly. So put many things aside that are unnecessary to God's Kingdom and dwell upon this as you go day by day doing the essential things at this time. And it is good that you do and continue keeping your home, working, day by day. But many things can be dropped aside. Watch your priorities, let them be most important to the Kingdom of God and your place in it. He is preparing you. You are being prepared in many ways without your understanding yet, but you will. So work together as children preparing to go to their great home. He will be with you. Selah

Yes, my children. I am your Shepherd, follow me and you shall safely be in your Father's heavenly home. Great is He that has begotten you in His image. Trust and believe on Him for His wisdom is great and you shall be raised up unto many things of which you little dream of at this time. Be faithful unto the end. Let not your soul be faint. The strength shall be given you through each trial. You shall be lifted up. Blessings; my love is always with you. Peace be with you as you smoothly go through the great momentum of energies which seem so great upon you. I shall walk with you through the mountains and by the sea every minute.

JR

11/3/83
