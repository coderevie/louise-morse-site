Scripture Reading: 8th Chapter of John

Our blessings are with you, children of the Light. You are those following the Master, the Light of the World. You are coming more and more into the higher degrees of Light that shall manifest as spirit, brought forth to renew the Earth. That which is begotten of the Father and sent forth is the leavening-- multiples of atoms of energy, Light manifesting through the carriers of light-- God's Light in magnification. Light comes from the rate of vibration--the movement of Light through various frequencies of release--and it manifests through the vehicles prepared for it, growing ever brighter. The energies become the building blocks of the new world.

As the Master gave, you shall be free as you know the truth. This within you is that Spirit bearing witness to the truth, because you recognize that from which you came. It shall lead you out of the false suspension which has held you for a time, and shall release you into freedom to expand, to go out beyond limitations. You are coming to the more productive stages of your being, just as in physical growth. The infant becomes the child, then comes into adolescent stage when the productive powers increase and can then reproduce of itself (with the law of two coming together in love), growing through this process into a mature state of development. In the spirit there is a similar way: the soul moving through what might be comparable to the adolescent age, coming into line, into greater productivity. Then union with the Father brings forth great fruits of the spirit, magnification of the light in the God-self, the begotten bringing forth that of like kind in light, energies of creative purposese The spirit yields the increase. :

You cannot understand it all at this time, but you can get a glimpse of that greater magnification, the sun-burst from the energies bringing forth new life in creative force. You and your Father are working together as one. He knew it but you did not, for a time. Now you are coming into the realization of that purpose, coming in line with His will, consenting to let the Father do the works through youe This sets that contact in motion, in action, and the power of the Holy Spirit moves through you in new ways--the Father doeth the works through you.e You do not have to know how. You trust, being productive in His name, for His purposes, using that which God is.

Energy, in certain rates of vibration, becomes light as it increases in action, motion, purpose--in speed. As we have explained before, the motives in the heart are that which drives forth the light energies into action, in form. They are the power that builds, changes, or transforms with the energy created. The Father sends forth the energies saying, “Let there be light." This moves in greater rate of vibration as it changes frequency. As that light goes forth and the idea, the intelligence behind the plan goes forth, then that power turns the energies into form.and learns to intelligently govern form. This is the son brought into form out of the energies the Father oreated from the beginning. So, that begotten of Himself brings forth mentally, or projects ideas, turns energies into forms worlds, solar systems, kingdoms.

Thus it was that Lucifer began to mentally devise his own kingdom and set it up in mental form, shaping and moulding that which he would, then taking and influencing that which God brought in for another purpose, drawing it < into the snare of his mentally-devised kingdom (for, in free will, each one can do as he will), The Christ, begotten of the Father, is in line with the Father's will, therefore sends forth in obedience to the Father's will, in love of the Father. Being one with Him, he sees the plan, knows His will and moves in line with that to bring about this forming of worlds, or reforming, transforming, changing over, rebuilding. The motives that go forth from the heart, the intent and purpose then shape according to the will that is coming forth.

Be ye one in the Father's will, in the Christ will. In the Christ nature you are one with Him. Then there is no deception or stealing of power for ulterior motives, as is done by those born under the spell or delusions of Lucifer, with lies and deceits. Those born of the spirit of God or born anew through the Holy Spirit have the motives which will permit them to reshape and change, bringing about great changes in themselves and everything upon which they look, for the power of the Spirit moves through them. They see in line with the Father's will. Their motives are pure because they are in the nature of love.

Love and hate come from the same energies, but of different will. Hate is of the will of self and shall die. Love is of the will of the Father. Love is the greatest power and shall always overcome, transcend and be victorious over all else. Therefore the Earth shall be changed and a transformation shall teke place within each--a changing of the form of nature, because it is directed forth in love. Love is the power that frees, brings ascension, lifts the soul out of the prison state, because God is lovee God over-rules all, but each soul must come in line through desire to be in God's will, to open to God's lovee Let that be the motive which goes forth to change the picture, to actually visualize, use and direct energies into form, directed by the Father within.

First must come the change of heart within (the transformation within each one), that it be love, that its motive be in line with God's will. Then from the God-center can all things be oorrected, changed, made into new form. People who try to satisfy their curiosity through the intellect, or those seeking sensationalism through psychic phenomena are side=-tracked on different tangentse They will not get the key until they have a change of heart within them, a transformation within. Then, in that God-center, all things will work together for good; then the by-products will come. But the change of heart must take place first, and the perspective from that which opens the Christ nature. 411 this must be considered.

When searching for God, one must go straight to the center and be willing to get out of the way, that God take the throne. When the human self is put away, God is there, but, again, there cannot be two masters. The human cannot rule if the Christ nature of the Father is to rule. The Christ nature is begotten of the Father in His image. It is God Light, God Love, Spirit of God in ean extension of Himself. The Father and the Son literally are one. That which you give forth from yourself, of yourself, is one with you, is of the same substancee God's creative force in various degrees is energy in various velocities, going forth as love from the heart--light, intelligence and wisdom, consciousnesse The consciousness, awareness and light must unite in purpose with the heart, the motives and the power which transforms. The heart must be in line with the consciousness and the will. These must be of like purposee Thus united there is wise and balanced power in action, governing power over the forms sent forth.
