If you hunger and thirst after the things of Spirit, we invite you to drink deep of the truths to be found herein. These lessons have been a great blessing to many, going out to several countries, as well as all across the States, for the past fourteen years. They have brought many into a truly Christian life, and an acceptance of the salvation bought for us by our Lord, Jesus Christ. The foundation of the work is the Holy Bible.

As the conscious, subconscious and the super-conscious, as man has called it, come into one great open light of recognition that all that is reality is of God and is One - then the purpose is revealed and the soul true to its dedication and conviction moves forward to fulfill the plan of the Father. But the human ways must be set aside.

As you have been brought into this great area of activity of the Fourth Dimension - Paradise State (which you shall soon see with the inner eye while yet in the physical consciousness), you have partaken of the love and understanding and blessings of the fellowship of those gathered together in His Name, on the physical plane. Many seeking, hungering, thirsting will be drawn to the Light, to the wells of Water of Life, that the Spirit may move through and transform into readiness those to be lifted and changed in the twinkling of an eye. Great things await you; great joy awaits you. Let your soul, even now, be filled with love and thanksgiving and joy, for of this is hope, and of hope is God. This is your lifting power.

Put down the old self that the new birth come into the cradle prepared, and all shall rejoice as we return many souls to that point from which they deviated, as we lead them with the Father's Power and Love - lead them to the door, that they may enter again through the Portals of Light into Paradise, to fulfill the mission for which they came.

We change vibrations that we may come into that atmosphere, that vibration, that wavelength in which we may meet your consciousness as it is speeded up and accelerated in its path of swirling motion - energy on its way to God.
